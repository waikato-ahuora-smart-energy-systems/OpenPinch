# Application

## Overview
Use-case orchestration for OpenPinch. `PinchProblem` loads, validates and prepares one case and runs targeting through its `target`, `design`, `plot` and `components` accessors; `PinchWorkspace` manages several named cases; `commands` exposes both as JSON-in/JSON-out commands for browser and backend engines. The remaining modules are service functions and derivations that these classes call. Private subpackages hold the problem internals and the workspace helpers.

## Interface
- `PinchProblem` (`problem.py`): load, validate (`source_validation_report` reports on a source without raising), prepare, target (via accessors), summarise (`summary_frame`, `metrics`, `report`), export Excel, compare, update options and `dt_cont` multipliers, derive residual and utility-transfer cases, show the dashboard.
- `PinchWorkspace` (`workspace.py`, in `__all__`): named cases, scenarios, active-case delegation, batches (`cases(...)`), comparison, bundle save and load.
- `CaseBatchResult` (`workspace.py`): frozen dataclass of per-case `results` and `errors` mappings.
- `run_command`, `run_command_json`, `MAX_BATCH_CASES`, `UnknownSessionError` (`commands.py`): engine commands over fresh problems or stored sessions; `run_command_json` is the Pyodide entry point.
- `data_preprocessing_service`, `direct_heat_integration_service`, `indirect_heat_integration_service`, `direct_heat_pump_service`, `indirect_heat_pump_service`, `direct_refrigeration_service`, `indirect_refrigeration_service`, `direct_orc_service`, `power_cogeneration_service`, `exergy_targeting_service`, `area_cost_targeting_service`, `energy_transfer_analysis_service` (`targeting.py`, in `__all__`): zone-level services; `data_preprocessing_service` builds the zone tree from a `TargetInput`, and the others take a prepared `Zone` and optional args, add targets, and return the zone.
- `calculate_heat_recovery_dt_min`, `calculate_all_period_heat_recovery_dt_min` (`heat_recovery_dt_min.py`, in `__all__`): inverse dt_min for a requested recovery, one period or every period.
- `calculate_hpr_load_kinks`, `calculate_all_period_hpr_load_kinks` (`hpr_load_kinks.py`, in `__all__`): vertices of the heat pump sink or refrigeration source net load profile and the logical HPR load fractions, one period or every period.
- `calculate_hpr_refrigerant_screen` (`hpr_refrigerant_screen.py`): refrigerant screen of every layout of a Carnot target's levels or explicit levels.
- `is_screened`, `plan_fluid_set_runs`, `run_fluid_sets` (`hpr_fluid_set_runs.py`): plan and run one vapour compression target per fluid set a refrigerant screen or layout proposes.
- `run_problem_utility_placement`, `build_problem_placement_context` (`utility_placement.py`, in `__all__`): optimise a utility set and return it as a new solved `PinchProblem`, or build the detached `(blueprints, context)` pair.
- `with_utilities_from` (`case_derivation.py`): receiver problem with the donor's utilities, aligned by period identity.
- `with_heat_pump_streams` (`case_derivation.py`): a new problem holding the problem's streams and a heat pump target's: a direct target's condenser, evaporator and ambient source streams in a process zone, or a utility target's condensers and evaporators as two process zones of the site, each stream marked with its machine, role and placement.
- `retrofit_with_heat_pump(problem, existing, settings, *, configuration=None, limits=None, machine="HP", sizes=(None,))`, `heat_pump_step`, `heat_pump_machine`, `heat_pump_case`, `joined` (`heat_pump_retrofit.py`): the retrofit search of an existing network together with a second search started from its as-piped heat pump added as streams, as one `HeatExchangerNetworkRetrofitResult`. `heat_pump_step` solves the as-piped target on the base design's coolers and heaters, shifted at `settings.dt_cont_multiplier` as the search's model holds them, with the search's cycle (`settings.heat_pump_cycle`, "carnot" or "vapour_compression"; `limits`: the heat pump search budget keys) and returns the `HeatPumpStep` (condensers, evaporators, the air the evaporators draw on, the machine) with the target, or `(None, None)` when there is no target; `heat_pump_machine` prices the machine (electricity: work at `COSTING_HPR_ELE_PRICE` over `COSTING_ANNUAL_OP_TIME`; compressor: `settings.heat_pump_cost` per kW of condenser capacity, or for "vapour_compression" the HPR service's installed machine without its process and air exchangers (compressors, motors, cascade exchangers and IHXs), `hpr_machine_internal_capital_costs`, annualised by the capital recovery factor); `heat_pump_case` is the problem with the machine's streams at `HPR_DT_CONT`, marked `heat_pump` (written over the zone's multiplier and over `dt_cont_multiplier`, which `retrofit_with_heat_pump` passes from `settings.dt_cont_multiplier`, so the retrofit's model, which scales every contribution by both, holds them at `HPR_DT_CONT` and the air at its own contribution); `sizes` are shares of the heaters' load (`load_fraction` of `heat_pump_step`, `HPR_LOAD_FRACTION` of the target on a copy of the configuration; `None` leaves the configuration's), one step and one second search per size, the share recorded on `NetworkHeatPump.load_fraction`; a size whose target, case or search raises `ValueError` (a target with no machine the cycle can model that pays among them) is left out with a `UserWarning` naming it and the others still count. `joined(base, *sized)` keeps at each number of steps the design with the best net annual benefit (a heat pump step is its search's first step), adds the evaluated designs and the failed solves step by step and marks dominated designs again. The second search runs under `configuration` on a snapshot of the case. Levels the target leaves without duty are left out of the step and the case; the case writes the streams at `HPR_DT_CONT`, and the air the evaporators draw on at its own contribution, over the dt_cont multiplier of the process zone they join. With no target, or `max_modifications` below 1, the result is the first search's; when building the case or the second search raises `ValueError` (process components, a site of several process zones, a network that cannot serve the machine's duties), it is the first search's with a `UserWarning`. `joined` sizes the failed solves by the longer of the two series, since a search that stops early counts its last, empty depth. The first search runs with `heat_pump_cycle=None`: the stream step replaces the linked heat pump move. On a problem with several periods the as-piped target is one machine designed for every period's coolers and heaters (`as_piped_heat_pump`): `heat_pump_step` takes as levels those carrying duty in any period, each from the first period it runs in, with `HeatPumpStep.in_periods` holding each level's stream in every period (`None` where it is off) so its matches hold their approach in every period it runs; `heat_pump_machine` (given `period_weights`, the master zone's period weights) sizes the machine on the largest condenser and evaporator duty of any period, and a "vapour_compression" machine's internal capital on each of its machines' largest of any period's `hpr_machine_internal_capital_costs`, and draws each period's work at its share of the weights; and `heat_pump_case` writes each stream in every period through the shared-design path of `_case_with_heat_pump_streams`, segment by segment for a segmented level, with the air of any period (the step's `ambient_sources` and `in_periods` hold it per period too, built from the problem's configuration where the solve's own period draws none). `retrofit_with_heat_pump` refuses any cycle but "carnot" and "vapour_compression". `design.retrofit` calls it whenever `heat_pump_cycle` is "carnot" or "vapour_compression", with `limits` `{"maximum_evaluations": 2000}` unless `heat_pump_search` is given (`{}`: the full search), with "vapour_compression" adds `HPR_N_COND=2` and `HPR_N_EVAP=1` to `configuration` for each that neither the problem's input options nor the retrofit's `options` name. The retrofit search's linked heat pump move, which `heat_pump_streams=False` chose, was retired on 2026-11-24; the keyword is gone.
- `heat_pump_design_case`, `HeatPumpDesignCase`, `heat_pump_request`, `design_with_heat_pump`, `heat_pump_design_view` (`heat_pump_design.py`): the case a heat pump network is designed on at one dt_cont multiplier (the target solved for that multiplier, the problem with its streams, the machine's priced record); the `heat_pump=` argument of a design as keyword arguments; the design without a heat pump and, at each multiplier, with one; and the one view that ranks them.
- `heat_pump_stream_records(case, machine)` (`case_derivation.py`), `heat_pump_network_case(problem, network)`, `with_heat_pumps(view, heat_pumps)` (`heat_pump_design.py`): the stream input records a heat pump case added for a machine (those of `case.to_problem_json()["streams"]` marked with it); the case a heat pump network was designed on, built from `problem` and the streams the network's machines carry (`NetworkHeatPump.streams`), each stream joining its zone under `problem`'s own root zone (a zone address starts with the project name, which may differ), `None` when the machines carry none or `problem` holds them all by zone and name; and a design view with machines recorded on every network of it, their annual cost in its total, ranked again. A process heat pump's record carries its streams from `heat_pump_design_case` and from `retrofit_with_heat_pump` (set on the step's record once its case is built: the records named for the step's own condensers, evaporators and ambient sources, so a machine added to a case that already holds one of the same name does not carry that one's streams); a utility heat pump's carries none.
- `level_split`, `placed_case`, `run_level_counts`, `select_level_count`, `level_sweep_view`, `site_cost`, `LevelCountRun` (`site_levels.py`): the utility level count sweep of a total site network design: each count's split, its placed case, one design per count, the cheapest count and the swept view.
- `movable_levels`, `shifted_case`, `level_duties`, `move_seed`, `refine_levels`, `refine_selected`, `LevelMove` (`site_level_moves.py`): level-temperature moves on a placed total site design: its placed levels, a case with one level moved, each process's re-targeted level duties, the seed a move is designed from, the moves on one design, and the sweep's cheapest count refined.
- `single_period_case` (`case_derivation.py`): a new single-period problem holding one period's data alone, keeping its period id with weight 1; the basis a HEN is synthesised from before it is fitted to every period.
- `residual_basis`, `create_hpr_residual_case`, `residual_utility_service`, `prepare_residual_problem` (`residual_utility.py`): frozen HPR residual cases.
- `hpr_result_digest`, `retain_hpr_target`, `validate_hpr_target`, `select_hpr_graphs` (`hpr_selection.py`): identify local HPR targets and select their graphs.
- Subdirectories: [_problem](./_problem/README.md) problem internals (input, periods, targeting, output, accessors); [_workspace](./_workspace/README.md) case-input and case-state helpers for `PinchWorkspace`.

## Invariants
- Getters on `PinchProblem` return deep copies or `MappingProxyType` views [problem.py:PinchProblem.results] [problem.py:PinchProblem.master_zone] [problem.py:PinchProblem.period_results]; `process_components` and `components.inventory` return a read-only mapping of the live component objects [problem.py:PinchProblem.process_components] [component.py:_ComponentAccessor.inventory].
- Changing options or a multiplier rebuilds the prepared state and clears cached results; process components are kept by re-solving them on the rebuilt tree, and a failed re-solve raises and restores the prior state; loading a new source replaces components with those the source saves, in `process_components`, and `to_problem_json`, `validate`, `problem_data`, bundles and `scenario` carry them; changing `project_name` clears cached analysis but keeps components [loading.py:rebuild_problem_state] [problem.py:PinchProblem.project_name] [problem.py:PinchProblem._invalidate_analysis].
- A frozen HPR residual case refuses `set_dt_cont_multiplier` [problem.py:PinchProblem.set_dt_cont_multiplier]; it replaces a non-finite or negative value by 1.0 with a `UserWarning`; the same value in a zone tree raises `ValueError` [canonicalization.py:_resolve_zone_dt_cont_multiplier].
- Changing `project_name` renames the root zone, and the new root name carries through later rebuilds and `to_problem_json` [problem.py:PinchProblem.project_name] [problem.py:PinchProblem._canonical_problem_inputs].
- Setting `PinchWorkspace.project_name` renames every live case and drops its cached results; cases built later take the name when they are built [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.project_name] [state.py:case_for_name].
- Period flags are bools and select one of `selected`, `weighted_average`, `all`, `all_with_weighted_average` [problem.py:_period_mode].
- Case names are validated by `validate_workspace_case_name`; a batch needs at least one unique name [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.load] [workspace.py:_CaseBatch].
- Once a batch is built, failures during each case's run are collected per case; the `target`, `target.all_periods` and `design` batch accessors refuse `base_target`; the `target` and `target.all_periods` batch accessors have no `hpr_performance_map` or `hpr_refrigerant_screen`, which take one case's target; Excel batch export confines each case to a subdirectory of the destination [workspace.py:_CaseBatchAccessor._run] [workspace.py:_CaseBatch.export_excel].
- `PinchWorkspace.load` stores a dict or `TargetInput` that fails validation or preparation so it can be corrected and returns `None` for it, otherwise the live case; a file or CSV source that fails to parse or validate raises and is not stored; it renames the workspace only for the first case or an explicit `project_name` [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.load].
- Bundles are written with schema version `"3"` [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.to_bundle].
- Commands never read host files: `{"sample_case": ...}` loads packaged cases only, workspace `source` must be JSON, and exports go to a private temporary directory [commands.py:_load_problem] [commands.py:_set_case_input] [commands.py:_export_excel].
- Command names starting with an underscore are rejected, and only `target` and `design` namespaces resolve to accessors [commands.py:_accessor_method].
- Command responses carry no NaN or infinity; they are replaced by `None` [commands.py:_finite].
- `workspace.batch` runs at most `MAX_BATCH_CASES` cases and cannot nest [commands.py:_batch].
- Heat flows for dt_min are canonicalised in kW (a bare number is taken in the configured heat-flow input unit); `heat_recovery` must be a finite, non-negative scalar, and exceeding the thermodynamic limit raises `ValueError` [heat_recovery_dt_min.py:_canonical_recovery] [heat_recovery_dt_min.py:calculate_heat_recovery_dt_min] [analysis/targeting/heat_recovery_dt_min.py:_validate_real_scalar].
- dt_min targeting and HPR load kinks are supported only for Site, Process Zone and Unit Operation zones [heat_recovery_dt_min.py:_resolve_zone_and_period].
- HPR load kinks read the direct-integration problem table of an isolated `snapshot_problem` copy, so the caller's targets, results and period state are unchanged; `steepening` must be a finite real number above 1 and `is_refrigeration` a bool, and a profile with no duty raises `ValueError` naming the scope and period [hpr_load_kinks.py:calculate_hpr_load_kinks].
- A refrigerant screen takes either a successful Carnot (cascade or parallel) heat pump or refrigeration target or both `T_evap` and `T_cond`, otherwise `ValueError` (`TypeError` for another target type); the target sets the topology from its cycle and heat pumping from its analysis method, and passing `topology` or `is_refrigeration` with it is a `ValueError`, while explicit levels need `topology` `parallel` or `cascade` (else `ValueError`) and are a heat pump unless `is_refrigeration`; levels are reported hottest first; a target must also be this problem's current target for its zone and period, with the current input fingerprint (`validate_base_target`), else `ValueError`; it supplies its distinct levels whose ports carry duty above `tol`, and explicit levels are one value or a sequence, in degC or with units. Thresholds come from the target's zone configuration (the root zone for explicit levels) with `options` applied for the call. The result's provenance names the target as its prerequisite. Nothing is stored on the problem [hpr_refrigerant_screen.py:calculate_hpr_refrigerant_screen] [hpr_refrigerant_screen.py:_placed_levels].
- A vapour compression target given a refrigerant screen or layout as `refrigerants` runs once per distinct (method, condensers, evaporators, MVR stages, fluid set) of the layouts for its method, in screening order, with the counts from the layout; given counts select layouts rather than override them; a screen for the other of heat pump or refrigeration, or no matching fluid set, is a `ValueError` before any run; a run that raises is recorded with its error and the rest still run; each run commits like a single call, so the problem keeps the last successful run [hpr_fluid_set_runs.py:plan_fluid_set_runs] [hpr_fluid_set_runs.py:run_fluid_sets] [_problem/accessors/target.py:_TargetAccessor._fluid_set_runs].
- Utility placement runs on isolated copies of the problem and checks that retargeted duties match the winning allocation (`rel_tol=1e-5`, `abs_tol=1e-4`) [utility_placement.py:_extract_period] [utility_placement.py:_finalize_placement_case].
- Utility placement scope follows the zone type (Process Zone and Unit Operation direct, Site total site, Community and Region indirect, Utility Zone unsupported); a frozen HPR residual case always uses direct scope [utility_placement.py:_scope_for_zone] [utility_placement.py:_build_problem_placement_request].
- Maximum duties are finite and non-negative, in the request's heat flow unit [utility_placement.py:_normalize_maximum_duties].
- A local HPR target is valid only if its owner id, input fingerprint and retained result digest all match the problem [hpr_selection.py:validate_hpr_target].
- A residual case is frozen to one period, and its utilities start with zero duty (segmented utilities carry duty fractions) [residual_utility.py:create_hpr_residual_case].
- `with_utilities_from` requires matching period sets and a `PinchProblem` donor, and does not copy donor results [case_derivation.py:with_utilities_from].
- `with_heat_pump_streams` adds each condenser stream of the target as a hot stream and each evaporator stream as a cold stream at the `HPR_DT_CONT` the target was solved with (its `provenance.effective_settings`, so a per-call `minimum_approach_temperature` or a zone's own setting is kept), keeping segments, so the new case's direct integration targets are the heat pump target's utilities; the air an evaporator draws on is added as a hot stream at its own contribution (`HPR_DT_ENV_CONT`), and the air a condenser rejects to is not (the case's cold utility takes that heat). The streams and the air are written over the dt_cont multiplier of the zone they join (`_zone_multiplier`), which scales them back to the approach the target used, as the retrofit's heat pump case and the site's heat pump zones write theirs; a zone whose multiplier is not positive raises `ValueError` in all three. Every added record carries `heat_pump: {machine, role}`. The streams go in the target's own process zone, the only process zone under it, or the one `zone` names by name or address. It raises `ValueError` for a problem with process components (they are not in the input records the new case is built from), a target that is foreign, stale or modified (`validate_hpr_target`), a target that is not a heat pump's (a refrigeration target), a problem with more than one period whose target is not a shared design (`hpr_shared_design`), a zone the target does not cover or leaves unnamed among several, and a zone that already has a stream of a heat pump stream's name [case_derivation.py:with_heat_pump_streams, _heat_pump_zone_address, _heat_pump_stream_record].
- Given a utility heat pump's target (`IndirectHeatPumpTarget`), `with_heat_pump_streams` adds the condensers as the hot streams of a process zone `"<machine> condenser"` and the evaporators as the cold streams of `"<machine> evaporator"`, both under the zone the target was solved on, each marked `placement: "utility"`. The new zones inherit that zone's dt_cont multiplier, so each stream is written with the target's `HPR_DT_CONT` over the multiplier and stands off a level by `HPR_DT_CONT` as the target used it. Two zones, because the streams of one zone recover heat from each other. A stream whose end lies inside the temperature band of a utility level no wider than heat pump targeting's `UTILITY_BAND_MAX_SPAN_K` (0.1 K) is moved to the band's end, with its segments: a condenser whose hot end less the approaches (`HPR_DT_CONT` plus the level's `dt_cont` times the zone's multiplier) is inside the band of a cold utility moves up to the band's top, an evaporator whose cold end plus the approaches is inside the band of a hot utility moves down to its bottom. The new case is then targeted on a copy (`indirect_heat_integration` of the same zone) and returned unsolved only when its hot utility is the target's and its cold utility the target's plus `hpr_ambient_cold`, within 1e-6 of the larger (at least 1e-6 kW). It raises `ValueError` otherwise (a level the heat pump delivers into that is not also a cold utility, one it draws on that is not also a hot utility, a stream part way through a wider band, an evaporator drawing on ambient air), and for `zone=`, a problem with more than one period, a target solved on a process zone, and a site that already has a zone of either name. Electricity and the compressor are not in the case's costs [case_derivation.py:with_heat_pump_streams, _case_with_utility_heat_pump_zones, _band_move, _require_heat_pump_site_target].
- `heat_pump_design_case` solves the heat pump target on a copy of the problem whose root zone multiplier is scaled by the multiplier, with the heat pump's approach (`levels`' `minimum_approach_temperature`, else `HPR_DT_CONT` in its `options`, else the problem's `HPR_DT_CONT`) times the zone multiplier and the multiplier, and writes its streams into the problem itself at the unscaled approach without the stale-target check (the target belongs to the copy), so that targeting the case with every contribution at the multiplier gives that target's utilities. Its record prices the target's work and the compressor (`cost_per_kw`) at the `costing` group it is given, the one of the design the case is for, or the problem's stored one without it [heat_pump_design.py:heat_pump_design_case] [case_derivation.py:_case_with_heat_pump_streams].
- `heat_pump_design_case` moves each condenser and evaporator stream that starts or ends within `HEAT_PUMP_SNAP_K` (1e-4 K) of a shifted temperature a process stream or segment starts or ends at, on the problem scaled to the multiplier, onto that temperature. A converged search leaves a level up to about 1e-5 K off the kink it belongs on: within 1e-6 K under it the problem table merges the two and the 0.01 K stream gains 1e-4 of its duty, and further under it the process stream's last sliver falls to the utility and makes a second pinch no design closes. The stream moves whole (segments too), each period by its own amount against that period's own process temperatures (a stream given one value for every period, against those of every period), the first of its temperatures that lies beside a process temperature setting the move, one already on a process temperature keeping the stream where it is, so the span the table reads its duty over is kept; a temperature beside no process temperature is left as the target gave it, to the last digit (a level between two others sits at its approach limit). Air streams are not moved. `with_heat_pump_streams` and the retrofit's `heat_pump_case` do not move streams. [heat_pump_design.py:_on_process_temperatures]
- `heat_pump_design_case` writes the air an evaporator draws on at the air's own contribution over the zone multiplier and the design's multiplier (`ambient_scale`), so the design, which scales every stream's contribution, holds the evaporator `HPR_DT_ENV_CONT` under the air as the target did, at any positive multiplier; at a multiplier of 0 no contribution survives and the air is written as it is. - With `placement="utility"` (`heat_pump_design_case` and `design_with_heat_pump`) the heat pump heats and cools the site's utility levels: the target is solved with that placement and its condensers and evaporators are written as the site's `"<machine> condenser"` and `"<machine> evaporator"` zones (`_case_with_utility_heat_pump_zones`, given the approach times the root zone multiplier, which it writes over that multiplier); the case scaled to the design's multiplier must target as the heat pump did (`_require_heat_pump_site_target`, the check `with_heat_pump_streams` makes), else `ValueError`, which `design_with_heat_pump` turns into a `UserWarning` and a size left out. `design_with_heat_pump` then designs with `design.total_site_heat_exchanger_network`, each case at its multiplier alone (`HENS_DT_CONT_MULTIPLIERS` in its options, the site design having no multiplier argument), tries the loads of `_UTILITY_LOADS` (1.0, 0.75, 0.5) when none is given, one case per multiplier and load at one approach, `levels`' `minimum_approach_temperature` or else `HPR_DT_CONT` (only the dt_cont multiplier varies it, as for a process heat pump), and on a problem with several periods targets a shared design (`HPR_MULTIPERIOD_OPTIMIZATION_ENABLED`, as for a process heat pump), whose zones `_case_with_utility_heat_pump_zones` writes per period from `_period_heat_pump_streams`, each period's stream moved within its level band on its own (`_moved_record` takes one move per period). `network_with_heat_pumps` adds the machine's annual cost to `summary_metrics["site_total_annual_cost"]` as well as to `total_annual_cost`, so a site network ranks with it.
- On a problem with several periods, `_case_with_heat_pump_streams` writes each condenser and evaporator stream with its temperatures and duty in every period, read from the shared-design target's `hpr_details.period_outputs` in the problem's period order (the target's own stream collections hold one period, which may be one the machine is off in, so each stream is taken from the first period it runs in); a period the machine is off in, left out of the period outputs or present without streams, gives the stream no heat flow at the temperatures of the first period it runs in; a segmented stream is written segment by segment on profiles aligned across the periods (`_aligned_segments`): each running period's segments, in order, are one temperature-heat profile, cut at the union of every period's cumulative duty fractions (`align_temperature_heat_profiles`), since segment names are positional and periods can break a profile at different duties; every period then has the same segments, each chaining end to end, a segment's film coefficient is that of the first running period's segment it lies in, and a period the stream is off in has no duty, at the first running period's temperatures; the air the evaporators draw on is role `"ambient_source"`, written in every period at that period's `Q_amb_hot` (no duty where none is drawn), a copy of the target's own hot air stream, or, where its own period draws none, the hot air stream the heat pump service builds from the target's `config` (`get_ambient_air_stream`: latent at the ambient temperature over `THERMAL_DT_PHASE_CHANGE`, at `HPR_DT_ENV_CONT`); `heat_pump_design_case` turns `HPR_MULTIPERIOD_OPTIMIZATION_ENABLED` on for the target, passes the problem's period weights to the record, and gives the record the load fraction it was asked for when the target reports none; `design_with_heat_pump`'s sizes are the logical load fractions of every period (`target.all_periods.hpr_load_kinks`) [case_derivation.py:_period_heat_pump_streams, _period_thermal_record, _require_heat_pump_stream_support] [heat_pump_design.py:heat_pump_design_case, _load_fractions].
- `heat_pump_request` takes a cycle name or a mapping with `cycle` (`"carnot"` when left out), `load`, `levels`, `cycle_options`, `search`, `options` and `machine`; another key raises `ValueError` naming it and anything else `TypeError`. `design_with_heat_pump` first refuses a problem heat pump streams cannot be added to (process components), before any design runs or the stored design changes, then designs the problem as `design.heat_exchanger_network` would without a heat pump, then at each multiplier and size builds the heat pump case and designs it at that multiplier alone; the sizes are the request's `load` alone, or without one every load fraction of `target.hpr_load_kinks().logical_load_fractions` (the step and steepening kinks of the net load profile, then the full load) on the copy of the problem scaled to that multiplier, since scaling the contributions moves the kinks; a multiplier and size whose target raises `HPRTargetingError` or whose design raises `WorkflowContractError` is left out with a `UserWarning`, and a `cost_per_kw` of 0 warns that the compressor is free. `heat_pump_design_view` leaves out every network of a heat pump design that puts more than `max_exchangers` exchangers with duty on one condenser or evaporator stream, found by its own key in the case's master zone (`capped_heat_pump_streams`; an ambient source is not capped, and a namesake in another zone is not counted) (`HENS_HEAT_PUMP_STREAM_EXCHANGERS`, 2 by default, from the call's effective configuration) and warns with the count left out and the count ranked; the cap is applied to the designed networks, not inside the design models, so a size can lose every network to it. It gives every network it keeps the case's record and annual cost (`network_with_heat_pumps`), sets each outcome's objective to that total, and ranks them with the plain design's networks in the plain view's result, which it returns with the cheapest selected; the problem's stored design stays the plain one, whose networks name only streams the problem has [heat_pump_design.py:heat_pump_request, design_with_heat_pump, heat_pump_design_view].
- `design.heat_exchanger_network(heat_pump=..., heat_pump_cost=...)` prices the heat pumps with the costing group its `options` give for the call (the configuration its networks are costed with), refuses `record=` (a record collects one run), and with `zone=` designs the zone's own case with its own heat pump [_problem/accessors/design.py:heat_exchanger_network].
- `_zone_case` gives the derived case the source problem's prepared utility streams (defaults included and still marked as defaults) rather than re-preparing them from the zone's streams, and keeps the zones above the zone by its ancestry, so a root name may contain "/" [case_derivation.py:_zone_case].
- A level count is at least 2 and splits into isothermal and sensible levels: the case's sensible utilities (other than HU and CU: segments, a profile, or a temperature change above `MINIMUM_SLICED_RANGE`, 1 K, in some period), counted on the side with more of them (a `Both` utility counts on each side), as utility placement infers its counts, stay sensible while two isothermal levels remain [site_levels.py:level_split]. `placed_case` places the count as generated pairs (each level serves heating and cooling) over every period of the site, gives the case those utilities with `with_utilities_from`, and renames each pair's hot and cold utility to one name (`hot_iso_1` and `cold_iso_1` to `iso_1`) so the site accounts it as one main. Where placement fell back on its own `HU` or `CU`, the case keeps instead its own utility of that side nearest the fallback by supply temperature (the one placement priced it as; `HU`/`CU` of the case's own are not candidates), one for each supply a period placed that fallback at, matched at that period's temperatures (the first period's when the period is not the case's), at its own temperatures and price, and `NoFeasiblePlacementError` is raised when the side has none. A placed level whose temperatures all lie within `MINIMUM_LEVEL_GAP` (1 K) of an earlier placed level's is dropped, as the two are one main [site_levels.py:placed_case, _fallback_utilities, _distinct_levels, _stand_ins].
- `run_level_counts` runs the counts in order; a `UtilityPlacementError` records the count `"infeasible"`, any other `ValueError` or `RuntimeError` from its design `"failed"`, a missing synthesis solver or dependency is raised, and with `early_stop` the counts after two designed counts in a row that cost more than the cheapest before them are `"not run"`; with `expired`, once a count is designed, each later count starts only while `expired()` is false and is otherwise `"not run"` with message `"time limit"`; a designed run keeps the case it was designed on. A count's cost is `summary_metrics["site_total_annual_cost"]` (site utility accounting) when the network reports it, else its `total_annual_cost` [site_levels.py:run_level_counts, site_cost].
- `select_level_count` takes the designed count of least cost (the first on a tie) and raises `WorkflowContractError` naming every count when none was designed; `level_sweep_view` returns that count's view with `ranked_networks` holding every ranked network of each designed count, its `objective_value` its site cost (`site_cost`) and its `summary_metrics["utility_level_count"]` the count, and provenance `design.total_site_heat_exchanger_network` on the root zone with `HENS_SITE_LEVEL_SWEEP` (one row per count) in its settings; it is not committed as the problem's design, since its networks name the placed case's utilities [site_levels.py:select_level_count, level_sweep_view].
- A placed level is a hot and a cold utility of one name matching `iso_<n>` or `sensible_<n>`. `shifted_case` moves both utilities' supply and target by the shift in every period and returns `None` when the level would touch or pass another placed level (by their temperature ranges) or leave the range of the site's stream supply and target temperatures [site_level_moves.py:movable_levels, shifted_case].
- `level_duties` targets each process zone alone (`_zone_case`) with every site utility at `nominal_approach_temperature` of the multiplier, as the level-held pinch design does, keyed by zone name then utility identity (`"Hot Utility.<name>"` for each of the zone's utilities, so a process of only hot or only cold streams is targeted too); `move_seed` keeps a network's active staged recovery matches and stage count with those duties as `site_processes` [site_level_moves.py:level_duties, move_seed].
- `refine_levels` first evaluates the unshifted levels as a move (logged `"baseline"`, shift 0; `"failed"` falls back to the start's cost), the re-design then stands for the unshifted levels (returned when nothing beats it, and the seed of the first moves), then tries every level up then down by the step each round, adopts the cheapest move whose site cost (`site_levels.site_cost`) is below the current one by more than 1e-6 relative, halves the step when none is, and stops below `step_min` (a step of 0 runs nothing) and returns the start's case and view when it costs no more than the result, ending the log with a `"start kept"` entry when moves were adopted; a `ValueError` or `RuntimeError` from a move's design records it `"failed"`, a missing synthesis solver or dependency is raised. Each level position (the full tuple of placed level temperatures, whatever the seed) is designed at most once: a move to one already designed is logged `"skipped"` with the cached cost and message `"already designed"`, or, when its design failed, no cost and `"already failed: <message>"`; a move `shifted_case` refuses is logged `"refused"` with the reason (two levels would touch or cross, or the level would leave the stream temperatures). With `expired`, it is checked before each design; once true the moves end with a `"stopped"` entry (message `"time limit"`) and the best so far is returned. `refine_selected` refines `select_level_count`'s run, replacing its case, view and cost [site_level_moves.py:refine_levels, refine_selected].
- `single_period_case` reduces every per-period quantity to that period's `value` with `period_source` (`analysis/heat_exchanger_networks/basis`), and rejects an unknown period and a problem with process components; the derived problem's targets equal that period's all-period targets [case_derivation.py:single_period_case].

## Side Effects
- `commands._SESSIONS` is module-level state holding live problems, workspaces and returned targets until the `close` command or process exit; each `open` and each returned case adds an entry [commands.py:_SESSIONS].
- While commands run, `warnings.showwarning` (and process-wide warning filters on interpreters without context-aware warnings) are replaced and later restored, guarded by a lock and a depth counter [commands.py:_collected_warnings].
- Commands read packaged sample data and the supplied file contents, and write export files only in temporary directories [commands.py:_load_problem] [commands.py:_import] [commands.py:_export_excel].
- `PinchProblem.load` can read JSON, Excel or CSV files from a path; `export_excel` writes a workbook; `show_dashboard` launches Streamlit [problem.py:PinchProblem.load] [problem.py:PinchProblem.export_excel] [problem.py:PinchProblem.show_dashboard].
- `PinchWorkspace.save_bundle` and `load_bundle` read and write a bundle file [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.save_bundle] [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.load_bundle]; `to_bundle` and `save_bundle` first sync live case edits into stored inputs [workspace.py:PinchWorkspace.to_bundle].
- `retain_hpr_target` writes `problem._retained_hpr_targets` [hpr_selection.py:retain_hpr_target].
- All-period dt_min and HPR load-kink runs may use a `ThreadPoolExecutor` [heat_recovery_dt_min.py:calculate_all_period_heat_recovery_dt_min] [hpr_load_kinks.py:calculate_all_period_hpr_load_kinks].
- `level_sweep_view` sets the selected view's result (its ranked networks, selection and provenance); it does not commit it as `problem._results.design` [site_levels.py:level_sweep_view].
- Utility placement mutates detached problem copies, and replaces `_period_results` and `_period_result_states` on the returned case [utility_placement.py:_finalize_placement_case].
- `with_utilities_from` replaces private fields on a snapshot when the receiver has process components [case_derivation.py:with_utilities_from].
- Optional imports (Streamlit dashboard, Excel export, residual and HPR analysis) are deferred to call time [problem.py:PinchProblem.show_dashboard] [problem.py:PinchProblem.export_excel].
