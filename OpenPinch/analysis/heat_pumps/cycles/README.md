# HPR Cycle Models

## Overview
Thermodynamic models of the heat-pump and refrigeration cycles that the
targeting modules in `../targeting` evaluate. A single closed vapour-compression
cycle (`VapourCompressionCycle`) is built on CoolProp; the cascade and parallel
networks aggregate several of them, the MVR classes model open vapour
recompression, and the Carnot and Brayton classes cover the idealised and TESPy
cases. Models return compressor work from `solve` and, for vapour cycles,
expose piecewise condenser and evaporator streams for the cascade accounting
in `../common`. `__init__.py` has only a docstring.

## Interface
- `VapourCompressionCycle` (`vapour_compression_cycle.py`): six-state single-stage cycle with an internal heat exchanger (gas-side change defaults to 10 K, 0 disables it), heat-pump or refrigeration mode, cascade duty hand-off, and `build_stream_collection`.
- `CascadeVapourCompressionCycle` (`cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py`): stages coupled through cascade exchangers; `solve` takes condenser and evaporator temperature arrays and a `DutyAllocationRequest`. Stages are ordered hottest first and heat always passes up the cascade. A heat pump solves from the top stage down and the coldest stage takes the remaining cooling; a refrigerator solves from the coldest stage up and the top stage rejects the remaining heat.
- `ParallelVapourCompressionCycles` (`parallel_vapour_compression_cycles.py`): independent subcycles solved side by side.
- `CycleLimits` (`limits.py`): the practical limits a vapour compression stage is held to, passed to `solve` as `limits`.
- `MechanicalVapourRecompressionCycle` (`mechanical_vapour_recompression_cycle.py`): open MVR stage with liquid-injection desuperheating, solved by `solve_from_source_heat` or `solve_from_mass_flow`; `process_heat_components` gives desuperheat, latent and subcool heat.
- `VapourCompressionMvrCascade` (`vapour_compression_mvr_cascade.py`): vapour-compression stages feeding a serial MVR train, whose stages always run on water (`solve` takes no MVR fluid); `MAX_MVR_STAGE_LIFT` limits stage lift; `eta_comp` gives the efficiency applied in each subcycle, vapour compression stages first.
- Compressor efficiency: `eta_comp` (vapour compression) and `eta_mvr_comp` (MVR) take a constant or a function of `CompressorConditions` (`../common/compressor_efficiency.py`), evaluated at each compressor's suction and discharge state; the cycles' `eta_comp` and `eta_mvr_comp` properties report the efficiency applied in the last solve.
- Motor efficiency: `VapourCompressionCycle.solve`, `ParallelVapourCompressionCycles.solve` and `CascadeVapourCompressionCycle.solve` take `eta_motor` (default 1.0, in (0, 1]); `VapourCompressionMvrCascade.solve` takes `eta_vc_motor` for its vapour compression stages and `eta_motor` for its MVR stages. `work`, `w_net` and the COPs are electrical (shaft work divided by `eta_motor`); duties are unchanged. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle]
- `CascadeCarnotCycle`, `ParallelCarnotCycles` (`carnot_cycles.py`): Carnot-family heat pump, heat engine and heat recovery stages with second-law efficiency factors.
- `SimpleBraytonHeatPumpCycle` (`brayton_heat_pump.py`): TESPy Brayton model (experimental, no `__all__`); `solve`, `get_hp_th_profiles`, `get_hp_hot_and_cold_streams`, `build_stream_collection`.
- `_MultiVapourCompressionCycleBase` (`_multi_cycle_base.py`): private base aggregating duties, temperatures and COPs over `subcycles`; `__all__` is empty.
- Subdirectories: none.

## Invariants
- The `Hs`/`Ss`/`Ts`/`Ps`, profile, COP and stream accessors raise `RuntimeError` until `solved` is true (`state_points` and `cycle_states` do not). Duty and work accessors of the single cycle return `None` or stale values before a solve; the cascade, MVR and VC+MVR `work` returns a finite infeasibility work and the cascade and parallel `penalty` returns 0.0 while unsolved (VC+MVR `penalty` is a list of infeasibility terms). The multi-cycle duty accessors (`Q_heat`, `Q_cond`, `Q_evap` and their array forms) and the parallel `work` raise `RuntimeError` while unsolved, yet `ParallelVapourCompressionCycles.solve` returns `sum(cycle.work)` even when `solved` is false. The COP accessors (`COP_o` on multi-cycle models only) raise `ZeroDivisionError` when the work they divide by is at most 1e-9 (specific net work for a single cycle, total work for multi-cycle, MVR and VC+MVR models). The Carnot accessors never raise; Carnot `solve` raises `ValueError` for inconsistent stage array sizes. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._require_solution] [_multi_cycle_base.py:_MultiVapourCompressionCycleBase._require_solution]
- Cycle temperatures are given in degC, held internally in K, and reported in degC after solving; for the CoolProp vapour cycles, state points hold `H`, `S`, `P`, `T` in SI units (the Brayton cycle uses its own state keys). [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve] [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._save_cycle_state]
- A single cycle has `STATECOUNT = 6` states; the MVR cycle has 4. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle] [mechanical_vapour_recompression_cycle.py:MechanicalVapourRecompressionCycle]
- `solve` raises `ValueError` for an evaporating temperature below the refrigerant's `Tmin`, an evaporator pressure above the condenser pressure, or a condenser with no enthalpy drop. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve]
- A compressor efficiency function outside (0, 1] raises `CompressorModelError`, not `ValueError`, so the targeting search does not read it as an infeasible point; a `ValueError` from the function itself propagates as one. With a constant efficiency no compressor conditions are computed. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._compute_compressor_outlet_state]
- A stage passing heat up gives the hottest of its condenser heat to the cascade exchanger and the coldest to its process duty. Subcooled liquid cannot heat the evaporator above, so subcooling heat must all fit in the process duty: `solve` raises the condenser outlet to that limit, without a penalty, and a stage with no process duty does not subcool. The internal heat exchanger's limit then follows the subcooling applied. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._coldest_outlet_serving_cascade_heat] [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve]
- A cycle is marked solved only when work is finite and non-negative. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve]
- Excess subcooling, missing IHX superheat and a two-phase compressor discharge (the gas short of its dew point) are returned as penalties scaled by mass flow, not as exceptions. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve]
- `CycleLimits` (`limits.py`) holds a stage to practical limits; `None` means no limit. Condensate is injected into the discharge until the gas is at most `max_discharge_superheat` above its dew point (subcritical only): the condenser rejects the same heat and the compressor does the same work, and the condenser profile runs from the capped temperature (`liquid_injection_ratio` reports the condensate per kg compressed). A stage above `max_pressure_ratio`, above `max_discharge_pressure` (`max_discharge_pressure_co2` for carbon dioxide), below `min_suction_pressure` or lifting less than `min_lift` is penalised by its condenser duty times the relative violation, so a stage carrying no flow is never penalised. The cascade, parallel and VC+MVR models pass `limits` to every vapour compression stage. [limits.py:CycleLimits] [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._limit_violations] [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._liquid_injection_ratio]
- The internal heat exchanger keeps a minimum hot-end approach `dt_ihx`: the suction gas leaves it at most `T_cond - dT_subcool - dt_ihx`, the liquid entering it less the approach. The cascade, parallel and VC+MVR models pass `dt_ihx` to every vapour compression stage; the working-fluid approach `dtcont` does not limit the IHX. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve]
- VC, Carnot, TESPy and MVR source/evaporator streams carry `delta_t_contribution = 0`; the MVR process-condenser streams (`MVR_process_H<n>`) carry the `dtcont` passed to `build_stream_collection`. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.build_stream_collection] [mechanical_vapour_recompression_cycle.py:MechanicalVapourRecompressionCycle._build_streams] [mechanical_vapour_recompression_cycle.py:MechanicalVapourRecompressionCycle._build_process_condenser_streams]
- Cascade solves return a finite infeasibility work, with `solved` left false, for temperature-order violations and unsolved subcycles; subcooling and IHX penalties on a solved cascade stay in `.penalty`. VC+MVR solves also return infeasibility work for input-bound penalties (stage-lift, split, subcool and approach). Wrong array sizes and missing duties raise `ValueError` once the temperature-order check passes. A bad refrigerant count raises in the cascade and parallel models, but VC+MVR pads or truncates the fluid list. `ValueError`s raised by a subcycle propagate from the cascade. [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle.solve] [vapour_compression_mvr_cascade.py:VapourCompressionMvrCascade.solve]
- A cascade with `T_cond` of size n and `T_evap` of size m has `n + m - 1` subcycles; condensing and evaporating temperatures should descend and every condenser should be `dt_cascade_hx` above every evaporator; a violation is penalised, not rejected: `solve` returns infeasibility work with `solved` false. [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._validate_T_cond_and_evap] [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle.solve]
- In a cascade only the balancing stage's `Q_heat` may be `None` or NaN: index `n_heat - 1` (the last of an `n_heat`-length input) in heat pump mode, index 0 in refrigeration mode. Only the last entry of `Q_cool` may be `None` or NaN, in both modes. [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._normalize_Q_heat] [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._normalize_Q_cool]
- In a cascade a scalar or one-element `Q_heat` applies to each of the `n_heat` process condensers; the stages below them deliver no process heat. In heat pump mode a scalar or one-element `Q_cool` applies to each process evaporator except the coldest, the coldest stage balances, and with one evaporator the value has no effect; in refrigeration mode it applies to every stage, except that with one process evaporator it applies to that evaporator's stage alone, the coldest. [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._normalize_Q_heat] [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._normalize_Q_cool]
- A `DutyAllocationRequest` `Q_base` on the primary side overrides its explicit `Q_heat` or `Q_cool`. [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._allocate_process_duties] [parallel_vapour_compression_cycles.py:ParallelVapourCompressionCycles._allocate_process_duties]
- Parallel cycles require every condenser temperature above its evaporator temperature, and a list of refrigerants must have one entry or one per cycle. [parallel_vapour_compression_cycles.py:ParallelVapourCompressionCycles._normalize_temperature_arrays] [parallel_vapour_compression_cycles.py:ParallelVapourCompressionCycles._normalize_refrigerant]
- `MechanicalVapourRecompressionCycle.solve` is disabled (`solve = None`); use the `solve_from_*` methods. Electrical work is shaft work divided by `eta_motor`. [mechanical_vapour_recompression_cycle.py:MechanicalVapourRecompressionCycle]
- In the VC+MVR cascade the last MVR stage always condenses all remaining vapour (`process_split` ends at 1), the first MVR stage takes its source heat from the top VC stage's condenser, and later stages receive the previous stage's uncondensed vapour mass flow. [vapour_compression_mvr_cascade.py:VapourCompressionMvrCascade._normalise_process_split] [vapour_compression_mvr_cascade.py:VapourCompressionMvrCascade.solve]
- Carnot cycles cap stage duties at the available evaporator duty without penalty and mark `solved` only if the energy balance closes within `tol` and entropy generation is non-negative. [carnot_cycles.py:ParallelCarnotCycles.solve] [carnot_cycles.py:CascadeCarnotCycle.solve]
- Cycle models in this directory depend on `../common` (encoding, shared helpers) but not on `../targeting`. [carnot_cycles.py] [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py]

## Side Effects
- CoolProp abstract states are created for each property lookup and temperature is root-found with SciPy `brentq`; `minimize_scalar` is used in the cascade Carnot solve. [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle._solve_single_phase_state_from_pressure_target] [carnot_cycles.py:CascadeCarnotCycle.solve]
- `solve` and `build_stream_collection` mutate the cycle object (states, duties, `dtcont`, `dt_diff_max`, cached condenser and evaporator curves). [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.solve] [vapour_compression_cycle.py:VapourCompressionCycle.build_stream_collection]
- The Brayton model imports TESPy optionally at module load, raises `ImportError` with install guidance when it is used without it, builds and solves a TESPy network, and issues a `UserWarning` when recuperation is requested. [brayton_heat_pump.py:_require_tespy] [brayton_heat_pump.py:SimpleBraytonHeatPumpCycle.solve]
- `_normalize_secondary_process_duty` sets the last element of a multi-element duty array to NaN in place, which can alter the caller's array. [cascade_vapour_compression_cycle.py:CascadeVapourCompressionCycle._normalize_secondary_process_duty]
- No file or network IO.
