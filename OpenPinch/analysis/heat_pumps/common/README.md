# HPR Common Calculations

## Overview
Calculation modules shared by the heat-pump and refrigeration (HPR) cycle
models, the per-topology targeting modules and the service entry point in the
parent package. They cover background-profile preparation, optimisation vector
layout and encoding, load selection, costing and result assembly, the shared
multi-stage vapour-compression objective, and residual utility accounting.
`__init__.py` has only a docstring.

## Interface
- `construct_HPRTargetInputs`, `default_refrigeration_to_power_ratio` (`preprocessing.py`): build `HeatPumpTargetInputs` (shifted, simplified background profiles, ambient flags, cost and cycle settings) from a zone's cascades and `Configuration`; default refrigeration electricity ratio.
- `resolve_hpr_target_load` (`load_selection.py`): requested HPR duty from `config.hpr.load_mode` (`fraction`, `duty`, `period_values`), capped by the available cascade duty in `duty` and `period_values` modes only.
- `find_hpr_load_kinks`, `HprLoadKinkProfile`, `LoadKinkVertex` (`load_kinks.py`): classify every interior vertex of the heat pump sink (`H_NET_COLD`) or refrigeration source (`H_NET_HOT`) net load profile as step, steepens, flattens or minor, and list the logical load fractions (step and steepens vertices, then 1.0).
- `HPRoptVectorLayout`, `clip_to_bounds` (`layout.py`): named sections of a flat optimisation vector (`pack`, `unpack`, `build_bounds`) and clipping of seeds into bounds.
- Duty and temperature encoding (`encoding.py`): `decode_available_fractions`, `encode_available_fractions`, `limit_available_duty`, `allocate_stage_duties`, `require_stage_duty_allocation`, `DutyAllocation`, `StageDutyRequest`, `DutyAllocationRequest` (with `from_state`), and the `map_*_to_*_arr` pairs between optimisation fractions and temperatures, temperature differences or duties.
- Component capital (`capital.py`): `CompressorDuty`, `MachineComponents`, `compressor_pec`, `motor_pec`, `exchanger_pec`, `installed_capital`, `matched_area`, `REFERENCE_MACHINE`, `COST_LEVEL`, `ANNEX_68_COST_AT_1_MW`.
- Machine components (`machine_capital.py`): `process_side_areas` (condenser, evaporator, air cooler and air heater areas of the solved heat pump streams) and `machine_components` (each machine's compressors and exchanger areas).
- Costing and results (`shared.py`): `calc_simulated_hpr_annualized_costs` (from each machine's installed capital), `hpr_penalty_cost_scale`, `refrigeration_allowance`, `heating_allowance`, `evaluate_carnot_hpr_result`, `evaluate_vapour_hpr_result`, `post_solve_error`; `cycle_limits` builds the `CycleLimits` for the vapour compression stages from the target inputs (the pressure ratio limit is a cycle penalty, not a decode clamp); `limit_parallel_pressure_ratios` raises evaporating temperatures and `limit_cascade_pressure_ratios` raises each lower level to meet `hpr.max_pressure_ratio`, for placing the warm start within the limit, and both do nothing for TESPy or when no refrigerants are set; constant `EVAPORATING_FLOOR_MARGIN_K`.
- Turndown floors (`shared.py`): `raise_parallel_lifts_to_floor` and `raise_cascade_lifts_to_floor` raise condensing levels until each stage lifts at least its floor; the decoders apply them when `args.stage_lift_floor` is set.
- Effective topology (`topology.py`): `effective_topology(model, args)` builds an `HPREffectiveTopology` from a solved cascade, parallel or VC+MVR model; `topology_from_stages(stages, args)` applies the same active-stage rule to a design given as `HPRActiveStage`s (a TESPy target).
- Search-range helpers (`shared.py`): `condensing_temperature_search_range` and `cap_stage_condensing_temperatures` (capped below the critical point for TESPy only; CoolProp stages above it run transcritical), `subcritical_stage_condensing_temperatures` (the per-stage cap, also used to seed every warm start subcritical), `floor_stage_evaporating_temperatures`, `validate_vapour_hp_refrigerant_ls`, `is_negligible_useful_duty`, and the constants `SUBCRITICAL_CONDENSING_MARGIN_K`, `NEGLIGIBLE_USEFUL_DUTY_FRACTION`.
- Thermodynamic helpers (`shared.py`): `calc_carnot_heat_pump_cop`, `calc_carnot_heat_engine_eta`, `compute_entropic_mean_temperature`, and `satisfies_second_law` (public but not in `__all__`).
- `_evaluate_multi_vc_objective` (`multi_vc_objective.py`): shared objective for cascade and parallel vapour-compression targeting (parse vector, solve, build streams, account, attach CoolProp record).
- `value_at_index`, `to_kelvin`, `to_degrees_celsius`, `to_kilopascal` (`units.py`): period-indexed value and unit conversion.
- Compressor efficiency (`compressor_efficiency.py`): `CompressorConditions` (refrigerant, suction and discharge pressures in kPa, `pressure_ratio`, suction temperature and dew points in degC, suction and saturated vapour densities in kg/m3), `compressor_efficiency_model` (a model name or function to a constant or a function of the conditions), `evaluate_compressor_model`, `CompressorModelError`, and the HeatPumpLab correlations `pareto_front_efficiency`, `wang_cleland_efficiency`, `brendel_efficiency`.
- `postprocessing.py`: only private functions; `_get_hpr_residual_utility_summary` is the one the service imports, retargeting utilities against the residual cascade after the HPR.
- Subdirectories: [_shared](./_shared/README.md): private air, stream and plotting helpers behind `shared.py`.

## Invariants
- Background profiles are shifted by `hpr.dt_cont` (hot temperatures down, cold temperatures up) and extended by 10 K at each end; `Q_cool_max` is taken as minus the last hot enthalpy, so the hot enthalpy profile is expected to be negative. [preprocessing.py:construct_HPRTargetInputs] [preprocessing.py:_extend_profile_with_temperature_margin]
- A refrigerator searches only the coldest `Q_hpr_target` of the cooling and is evaluated against the full cooling profile. [preprocessing.py:_split_cooling_profile_at_coldest_Q_target]
- A vapour heat pump's condenser heat rejected to air is a penalty term (wrong-side heat); a refrigerator rejects to air freely. With `enforce_heating_duty`, hot utility beyond the heating allowance (the full heating's hot utility less `Q_hpr_target`, at least 0, on the same cascade) is also a penalty term. [shared.py:evaluate_vapour_hpr_result] [shared.py:heating_allowance]
- The refrigeration allowance, the default refrigeration the warmer rest needs on its own, is the full cooling's need less `Q_hpr_target` (at least 0), on the cascade the designs are evaluated on, so the problem table's temperature rounding at the split cannot leave a design that serves its whole target short. [shared.py:refrigeration_allowance]
- A zero target raises `ValueError` for heat pumping [preprocessing.py:_get_reduced_bckgrd_cascade_till_Q_target] and, when the cooling profile exceeds the target, for refrigeration [preprocessing.py:_split_cooling_profile_at_coldest_Q_target].
- The default refrigeration price ratio is never below the cooling-water ratio. [preprocessing.py:default_refrigeration_to_power_ratio]
- Stage duties are decoded as fractions in [0, 1] of each stage's available duty, so no stage can request more than it can deliver. [encoding.py:decode_available_fractions]
- Load kinks walk the sink profile up from the pinch and the source profile down from it, after dropping repeated rows; slopes are `|dT| / d(Q/Q_max)`. A vertex followed by no duty is a step, and the step's top row is not listed again. Otherwise it steepens when `slope_after >= steepening * slope_before` (and `slope_after > 0`), flattens on the mirror condition, and is minor otherwise. `steepening <= 1` and a profile with no duty raise `ValueError`. Duty below `1e-9 * Q_max` and temperature change below `1e-9` K count as zero. [load_kinks.py:find_hpr_load_kinks]
- `HPRoptVectorLayout` section order is cond, evap, subcool, heat_split, cool_split, ihx, misc, and counts must be non-negative. [layout.py:HPRoptVectorLayout]
- The load is `Q_max * load_fraction` in `fraction` mode and is capped at the available duty `Q_max` in `duty` and `period_values` modes; an unknown mode, a missing `config` or a missing period key raises `ValueError`. [load_selection.py:resolve_hpr_target_load]
- A machine's installed capital is `COST_LEVEL * 4.16 * PEC`, the purchased equipment cost of its compressors (on suction volume flow), motors (on shaft power) and plate exchangers (on area), with Ommen et al. (2015) correlations; `COST_LEVEL` makes Ommen's 1 MW ammonia example cost `ANNEX_68_COST_AT_1_MW`. A flow beyond the largest compressor (280 m3/h; 25.6 m3/h for CO2) takes several equal ones in parallel, each with its own motor driving its share of the shaft power. Water compressors are turbo compressors anchored on Zuhlsdorf et al. (2018), one unit up to 280,000 m3/h, and, like hydrocarbon ones, include their motor. Fluids outside the families are priced as R134a. [capital.py:compressor_pec] [capital.py:motor_pec] [capital.py:installed_capital]
- Process-side areas are matched interval by interval (`U * LMTD`, the heat pump and process approaches added back) against the closest background window that the heat pump curve does not cross: condensers slide down from the top of the sink, evaporators up from the bottom of the source, and curves that cross everywhere need an infinite area. `U` follows the heat pump side's phase (`u_two_phase`, `u_gas`, `u_liquid`); a condenser section is gas or liquid against its own stage's condensing temperature (its isothermal section, or for a transcritical stage the highest condensing temperature within its range), and sections of several stages in one interval add as conductances. [machine_capital.py:process_side_areas] [machine_capital.py:_closest_area] [machine_capital.py:_condenser_coefficient] [capital.py:matched_area]
- Condenser heat to air and evaporator heat from air (the cascade's air duties) are sized against the ambient temperature with `u_air` in series and the air approach added back: the coldest part of the condensing curve, the warmest of the evaporating curve. Wrong-side heat has no exchanger, and a process duty below `1e-4` of the curve's heat is round-off. [machine_capital.py:process_side_areas]
- Each machine has its stages' compressors, IHXs (at `u_gas`, from their end temperatures) and cascade exchangers (at `u_two_phase` and `dt_cascade_hx`), and the process and air areas shared by its process heating or cooling. An MVR stage's suction volume comes from the water state at its suction pressure and enthalpy. [machine_capital.py:machine_components] [machine_capital.py:_compressor] [machine_capital.py:_internal_areas]
- The feasibility penalty is `rho * sum(r + r^2)` with violations relative to `Q_hpr_target`. Vapour results price it at the no-heat-pump annual cost, at least 1 $/y; Carnot results scale it by `max(Q_hpr_target, 1) * max(price ratio, 1)` in power-equivalent units, with violations measured against `max(Q_hpr_target, 1)`, with no annual-cost scale or floor. [shared.py:_cycle_penalty] [shared.py:hpr_penalty_cost_scale] [optimisation_adapter.py:build_hpr_accounting]
- Errors raised after a cycle has solved are re-raised, not ranked as infeasible candidates. [multi_vc_objective.py:_evaluate_multi_vc_objective] [shared.py:post_solve_error]
- Ambient air and cooling water are applied through `cascade_with_air` on the condenser and evaporator cascades; cooling water applies on the evaporator side only. Air is left out when `args.ambient_air_enabled` is false (`HPR_AMBIENT_AIR_ENABLED`). [shared.py:_cascade_air_duties]
- `calc_carnot_heat_pump_cop` and `calc_carnot_heat_engine_eta` take absolute temperatures (K); `satisfies_second_law` takes degC and adds 273.15 internally, and `compute_entropic_mean_temperature` takes degC by default and returns K. [shared.py:calc_carnot_heat_pump_cop] [shared.py:satisfies_second_law]
- Residual loads below `max(tol, 1e-7 * largest)` are treated as rounding noise and set to zero; duplicate residual temperatures with conflicting values raise `ValueError`. [postprocessing.py:_residual_noise_floor] [postprocessing.py:_deduplicate_residual_profile_rows]
- `HPR_COMPRESSOR_MODEL` and `HPR_MVR_COMPRESSOR_MODEL` `"constant"` pass `HPR_ETA_COMP` and `HPR_MVR_ETA_COMP` through as floats, so the default cycles are unchanged; only the vapour compression cycle types take a `HPR_COMPRESSOR_MODEL` model, and other types (Brayton passes `eta_comp` to TESPy) keep `HPR_ETA_COMP`; the named models are HeatPumpLab's correlations bounded to [0.4, 1.0] and pickle. [preprocessing.py:construct_HPRTargetInputs] [compressor_efficiency.py:compressor_efficiency_model]
- `common` imports the parent's `optimisation_adapter` and `performance_maps.targeting` for accounting and backend names. [shared.py] [preprocessing.py]

## Side Effects
- Imports CoolProp at module load; calls `PropsSI` for `Tmin` and `Tcrit` and `critical_point` for critical temperatures. [shared.py:floor_stage_evaporating_temperatures] [shared.py:validate_vapour_hp_refrigerant_ls]
- `refrigeration_allowance` and `heating_allowance` cache their result by setting an attribute on `args`. [shared.py:refrigeration_allowance] [shared.py:heating_allowance]
- `_get_hpr_residual_load_profiles` writes the post-HPR profile columns into the problem table passed in. [postprocessing.py:_get_hpr_residual_load_profiles]
- `construct_HPRTargetInputs` copies its array arguments before modifying them. [preprocessing.py:construct_HPRTargetInputs]
- Optional Plotly use through `_shared.plotting` when `debug` is set on a final evaluation. [shared.py:evaluate_vapour_hpr_result]
