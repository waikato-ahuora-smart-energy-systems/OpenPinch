# Total Site Heat Exchanger Network Design

## Overview
Helpers for designing one network for a site of several processes linked by
shared utilities. `seeds.py` joins each process's pinch design networks into
site seeds; `levels.py` reads the site's utility levels from its problem;
`utility_system.py` adds the site utility balance (imports, letdowns and
dumps per level and period) as rows and variables of the network's own
model, so the site pays only for its net utility use. There is no separate
accounting problem: the stage-wise model of a site task carries the block
(`../models/_stagewise/site_utilities.py`). The public entry point is
`design.total_site_heat_exchanger_network`
(`OpenPinch/application/_problem/accessors/design.py`).

## Interface
- `process_level_duties`, `site_seeds` (`seeds.py`): a process network's utility duties per level (its level targets when it holds them, otherwise its own heater and cooler duties), and site seeds (`site_seeds(pools, level_held=)`): per dt_cont multiplier in ascending order, a `"level_held"` seed from each process's networks in `level_held` that hold their level targets and a `"lowest_cost"` seed from its networks in `pools` (levels chosen by price), each taking per process the network with the fewest units (`summary_metrics["total_units"]`, else its active exchangers), then the lowest total annual cost, at that multiplier or the nearest one above it (the nearest at all only when it has none above; a process with no level-held network at or above the multiplier gives its lowest-cost one to both); seeds are made only at multipliers every process has a network at or above (all multipliers when none qualifies), and a process with no network raises `ValueError`, recorded as `summary_metrics["site_seed"]`, a seed equal to another dropped; each joins the recovery matches and the staged heaters and coolers (stage set, active in some period; end heaters and coolers are left to the evolution), the largest stage count and `source_metadata["site_processes"]` (each process's level duties); seeds without recovery are dropped, and `ValueError` is raised when none recovers heat.
- `joined_process_networks(pools, level_held=)` (`seeds.py`): for each choice `site_seeds` makes, the same process networks whole: every active exchanger of each, end heaters and coolers included, their capital cost added up, and what the seed records (`site_seed`, the multiplier, `site_processes`), a network equal to another in exchangers, level duties and capital cost dropped (two multipliers' networks of one structure and different areas are both kept). Its utility cost is left to the site balance.
- `SiteUtilitySystem`, `site_utility_system(solver_arrays, capacities=, letdown_price=)`, `utility_capacities(zone)` (`levels.py`): the site's `SiteUtilityLevels` (`OpenPinch/contracts/synthesis/site.py`) from its unsliced solver arrays, with the utility identities each level serves; `members(solver_arrays)` gives the level index of each hot and cold utility of (possibly sliced) arrays; `utility_capacities` gives each hot utility's `maximum_heat_flow` per period by identity. `priced_by_site_balance(network, system)` costs a network by the site balance of its own duties: each level's use from its heaters and generation from its coolers (by the utility each names), `utility_system.balance_of_loads` for the cheapest balance, `utility_cost` and `total_annual_cost` (capital plus that) set from it, and the site cost metrics and balance a site model's own network carries.
- `add_site_balance(m, levels, n, use, generation)`, `SiteBalancePeriod`, `letdowns`, `period_cost`, `solved_balance` (`utility_system.py`): add period `n`'s balance rows to model `m` given each level's use and generation as expressions of `m`; the period's handles and its import cost (`hot_cost`) and dump and letdown cost (`cold_cost`); the letdown pairs of a period; the solved costs of a period; and the solved `UtilitySystemBalance` (per level and period use, generation, import, letdown in and out, dump and cost; period costs, the weighted operating cost, and site hot and cold utility per period).
- Subdirectories: none.

## Invariants
- A hot utility (`"Hot Utility.<name>"`) and a cold utility (`"Cold Utility.<name>"`) of one name are one main (`"both"`) only when their temperatures match in every period (hot inlet with cold outlet and the reverse, within 1 K), as a `Both` utility splits; otherwise each is its own level, named `"<name> (hot)"` and `"<name> (cold)"` when the names clash. Prices are the arrays' annual prices ($/kW/y); temperatures are degC per period; a hot utility's `maximum_heat_flow` is its level's import capacity [levels.py:site_utility_system, utility_capacities].
- Per level and period: `use + letdown_out + dump == generation + letdown_in + imported`. Imports go only into hot and both levels, at the import price and within `import_capacity`; dumps leave only cold and both levels, at the dump price, and a level whose dump price is a credit (negative) dumps no more than its generation, so bought or let-down heat earns no credit; heat is let down from a level to another only when the first lies wholly above the second in that period (`t_low[n] >= t_high[n]`), at `letdown_price` [utility_system.py:add_site_balance, letdowns].
- The block is linear, so a linear model stays linear. Minimising its cost with the loads fixed gives the cheapest balance; with import prices rising with temperature and one dump price on every level, its cost and site hot and cold utility equal the Total Site cascade's (`OpenPinch/analysis/targeting/indirect.py:_target_site_utilities`), which a Hypothesis property checks under HiGHS.
- `operating_cost` is `sum(w_n * cost_n) / sum(w_n)` over the period weights, as the stage-wise models weight periods [utility_system.py:solved_balance].

## Errors
- A balance the levels cannot close (an import capacity too small) makes the model that carries it infeasible; the solver reports the failure.
- The contracts reject a level whose kind and prices disagree (a hot level needs `import_price` and takes no `dump_price`, a cold level the reverse, a both level both), non-finite prices or temperatures, `t_high < t_low`, duplicate level names, and values not one per period [`OpenPinch/contracts/synthesis/site.py`].

## Side Effects
- `add_site_balance` adds variables and rows to the model it is given. `balance_of_loads(levels, use, generation)` solves the same rows for fixed loads (per level name and period) as one linear programme per period with SciPy, so it needs no synthesis solver, and returns the solved balance; `ValueError` when a period cannot be balanced. The rest is pure.
