# HEN Equation Models

## Overview
Concrete equation models for heat exchanger network synthesis, plus the internal problem shell that builds, solves and reduces them. `BaseHeatExchangerNetworkModel` holds shared setup; `StageWiseModel` and `PinchDecompModel` are the superstructure models; `FixedStructureModel` allocates duty on a user-fixed network. `../execution/executor.py` and `../duty_optimisation.py` import from here, and `../extraction` converts solved models to network results. The package `__init__` is a docstring only, so import model modules directly.

## Interface
- `BaseHeatExchangerNetworkModel` (`base.py`): abstract base. The constructor stores options, creates the model (a native Pyomo `PyomoModel`, solved with `highs`, `couenne` or `ipopt-pyomo`), then calls abstract `setup`; also provides `optimise`, `cleanup`, `get_alpha_values`, `set_match_restrictions` and utility-cost helpers by delegating to `_base`. Subclasses implement `setup`, `set_preprocessing`, `set_stage_wise_superstructure`, `set_obj`, `get_post_process`.
- `StageWiseModel` (`stagewise.py`): TDM, ESM and amalgamated PDM model; delegates to `_stagewise` (evolution, verification, ranking, warm start). `staged_utilities=True` (default off) also offers a heater and a cooler at every interior stage boundary (`_stagewise/staged_utilities.py`); post-processing, extraction, the total-cost goals, verification, warm start and evolution children (whose staged units stay at the parent's flags) handle them, and `PinchDecompModel(staged_utilities=True)` offers them on its side with each utility level held at its target; stage reduction keeps a network that holds levels (`staged_utilities`) at its stage count. `level_targets` (`_stagewise/level_targets.py:UtilityLevelTarget`) holds a level's duty over a group of streams, and `stream_groups` (a label per hot and cold stream) allows recovery only within a group; evolution children inherit both, as a site network holds each process's levels and matches within processes.
- `PinchDecompModel` (`pinch_decomposition.py`): one pinch side; delegates to `_pinch_design`; `amalgamate_networks` joins two sides into a `StageWiseModel`; `used_units` and `exclude_units` read a solved side's unit pattern and cut it from another side model. A `highs` model that is not linear is solved with Couenne instead, or with SCIP when Couenne is not installed, without the HiGHS options except a time limit, and logs a warning. `require_units` sets a floor on a side's unit count.
- `StagePackedStageWiseModel` (`packed_stagewise.py`), `StagePackedPinchDecompModel` (`packed_pinch_design.py`): the above with stage-packing constraints added after the superstructure.
- `add_recovery_stage_packing_constraints` (`stage_packing.py`): forces active recovery stages to be the first of the stages with an allowed match (a stage with none is skipped, not a barrier to the stages after it) and imposes a minimum duty on each active match and stage.
- `InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem`, `ModelSliceUnavailableError` (retained, never raised), `FrameworkName` (`problem.py`): problem dataclass with `load_model`, `get_solution`, `remove_unused_stages`, `extract_network`, `extract_candidate_network` (any solved model of the task, such as an evolution candidate, with the task's arrays and labels), `extract_result`; `dt_cont_multiplier` (the synthesis task's) is passed to `extract_network`'s network.
- `FixedStructureModel`, `FixedStructureSpec`, `RecoveryMatch`, `UtilityMatch`, `ExchangerResult`, `ExchangerPeriodResult`, `FIXED_STRUCTURE_GOALS`, `HEATER`, `COOLER` (`fixed_structure.py`): fixed-network duty NLP and its specification and result types. Public functions: `prepare_fixed_structure`, `build_fixed_structure_equations`, `set_fixed_structure_objective`, `post_process_fixed_structure`, `initial_point`, `utility_chains`, `utility_free_components`, `utility_profile`, `stream_profile`, `stream_off`, `utility_side_temperatures`, `operating_cost_expression`, `capital_cost_expression`, `retrofit_capital_expression`, `fixed_unit_cost`, `chen_lmtd`, `exact_lmtd`.
- In the fixed-structure model's results an exchanger is active in a period when its duty exceeds 1e-4 of the smaller of its two streams' loads (a heater's or cooler's process stream's), never under the model tolerance: a small stream's exchangers beside a large stream are kept. [fixed_structure.py:_activity_floor]
- `ThermalProfile`, `SMOOTHING_K`, `NARROW_SEGMENT_SHARE`, `smooth_max0`, `smooth_clip` (`thermal_profiles.py`): piecewise heat-temperature profile with smooth solver expressions and exact reporting functions.
- Subdirectories: [_base](./_base/README.md) shared equation, area, cost and execution helpers; [_pinch_design](./_pinch_design/README.md) pinch-side preprocessing, equations, post-processing and amalgamation; [_stagewise](./_stagewise/README.md) stagewise preprocessing, equations, objectives, evolution, verification and warm start.

## Invariants
- A thermal derivative or evolution problem carries the share of each match's pinch design approach it holds (`approach_share`, from `HENS_MIN_EXCHANGER_APPROACH_SHARE`); every network it extracts records it as `source_metadata["min_exchanger_approach_share"]` and as the `summary_metrics` entry of that name (which the network's JSON keeps; `source_metadata` is left out of it), which a later fit, rating or overdesign reads in place of the configuration [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.extract_candidate_network] [../execution/executor.py:LocalSynthesisExecutor._build_problem].
- If `setup` raises, the model's run directory is removed before the exception propagates. [base.py:BaseHeatExchangerNetworkModel.__init__]
- `StageWiseModel.setup` order: blank parameters, solver-array parameters, preprocessing, match restrictions, superstructure, dQ/dA equations (TDM only), objective. `PinchDecompModel.setup` inserts `calculate_pinch` before preprocessing and has no dQ/dA step. [stagewise.py:StageWiseModel.setup] [pinch_decomposition.py:PinchDecompModel.setup]
- `FixedStructureModel` accepts only "total utility", "utility cost" (operating cost with no area model), "total area", "total cost", "rating" and "retrofit", and fixes `framework="ESM"`, `non_isothermal_model=True`, `integers=False`. [fixed_structure.py:FixedStructureModel.__init__]
- The "rating" goal fixes every exchanger's area at `FixedStructureSpec.recovery_areas` and `utility_areas` (its cap widened by `_RATING_SLACK`, 1e-6, so a unit rated at exactly its design duty has slack for the solver; `area * factor * U * Chen-LMTD >= q` in every period, `factor` from the design points `recovery_design_points` and `utility_design_points`: the required (slice) area over the Chen area at the design duty and approaches, or exact over Chen LMTD without one, default 1, so a period may bypass), minimises the period-weighted operating cost only, and reports the installed areas as the exchangers' areas and capital basis [fixed_structure.py:_installed_areas, post_process_fixed_structure].
- A recovery match idles in a period (`recovery_idle[n][r]`) when its hot supply is less than its approach above its cold supply there, or when `FixedStructureSpec.recovery_idle` holds it idle in that period. Its duty is a zero parameter, it has no branch, approach or interior equations, each stream leaves it at its inlet temperature, and its result's approaches are the difference the streams pass at (negative where the cold stream is the hotter; a solved network's period state floors it at zero, as every approach). A match too close in every period raises `ValueError` [fixed_structure.py:_idle_recovery_periods, _recovery_equations].
- `FixedStructureSpec.loops` holds heat recovery loops as (cooler, heater), the two by index in `utilities`; in every period the heater duty equals the cooler duty (no loss), for every goal [fixed_structure.py:build_fixed_structure_equations].
- `FixedStructureSpec.level_holds` holds utility levels as (utility indices, per-period targets): in every period the duties of those utility exchangers sum to within `LEVEL_BAND` (5e-4, relative to the target or of 1 kW, half the tolerance `holds_utility_levels` checks) of the target, a band rather than an equality so a level held at zero does not pin duties bounded at zero, for every goal [fixed_structure.py:build_fixed_structure_equations].
- The "retrofit" goal caps each unit with an installed area (an existing unit) as rating does, at that area times `1 + FixedStructureSpec.area_tolerance`; a unit in `enlargeable_recovery` or `enlargeable_utilities` adds a free non-negative added area to its cap, bounded above by `max_added_area_fraction` times its installed area when that is set; a unit in `enhanced_recovery` or `enhanced_utilities` has its whole capacity (installed plus added area) multiplied by its factor, as a raised heat transfer coefficient. A unit without an installed area (a new unit) gets a common design area as "total cost" does, all of it added. It minimises operating cost plus the annualised area cost (`COSTING_HX_AREA_*` law) of the added areas only; fixed costs are left to the caller. Each `ExchangerResult` carries `added_area`, its `area` is the installed plus added area (a new unit's design area), and `retrofit_capital_value` is the added areas' annualised cost. The goal imports duals when the model can (`PyomoModel.import_duals`); `area_values` maps each installed unit, keyed `("recovery" | "utility", index)`, to what one more m2 of installed area would save in the objective ($/y per m2: its caps' duals times their slopes in area, summed over periods), or is `None` when the solver reported no duals. `rating_allowances` (for the "rating" goal too) maps each installed unit the same way to the most, over its active periods, that its reported required area (exact LMTD, or summed slice areas for a segmented unit) exceeds the area its cap needs at that duty (duty over the cap's design-calibrated factor and Chen LMTD), at least 1 [fixed_structure.py:_rating_allowances] [fixed_structure.py:_retrofit_areas, retrofit_capital_expression, _reported_area, _area_values].
- Fixed-structure stages are 0-based; hot streams pass stages 0 to S-1 and cold streams S-1 to 0; utility exchangers at one position run in series (heaters coldest to hottest utility, coolers warmest to coldest). [fixed_structure.py:utility_chains]
- Fixed-structure validation raises `ValueError` for out-of-range indices, a recovery match whose hot supply is within its approach of the cold supply in every period (in some periods only, it idles there), a utility that cannot meet its approach at any duty, and utility-free stream groups whose loads differ. [fixed_structure.py:_check_indices] [fixed_structure.py:_idle_recovery_periods] [fixed_structure.py:_check_utility_reach] [fixed_structure.py:utility_free_components]
- A stream that is off in a period has its exchanger duties fixed at zero and its balances skipped. [fixed_structure.py:_period_variables]
- Solver areas use Chen's LMTD; reported design area uses the exact LMTD and is the maximum over periods. [fixed_structure.py:chen_lmtd] [fixed_structure.py:_design_area]
- `ThermalProfile` is frozen; its arrays must share one non-zero length and `side` must be "hot" or "cold". Smoothing applies only to symbolic arguments; plain numbers are exact. The smoothed `heat(T)` is exact at the supply and the target, so a stream's span in the fixed-structure model is its `total`, which bounds the model's recovery and utility duties. A boundary is rounded over `SMOOTHING_K`, or over `NARROW_SEGMENT_SHARE` of the narrower segment beside it where that is less (`rounding_widths`), so a condensing segment 0.01 K wide keeps its heat; the fixed-structure model clips a boundary into a match's range over the same width. [thermal_profiles.py:ThermalProfile.__post_init__] [thermal_profiles.py:smooth_max0] [thermal_profiles.py:ThermalProfile.rounding_widths]
- Stage packing applies only when `integers` is true, `S > 1` and some stage has positive capacity. A match's and a stage's duty and capacity are summed over every period (`Q_r_by_period`, `Q_max_period`), so a match that carries load only in later periods counts as active; a model without per-period duties uses `Q_r` and `Q_max`. [stage_packing.py:add_recovery_stage_packing_constraints, _period_duties, _period_capacities]
- PDM construction needs "above" and "below" decompositions; stage-wise construction needs an explicit stage count or a parent solution. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._build_pdm] [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._build_stage_wise]
- The amalgamated PDM case is built, not solved, so it takes the termination condition of the first required side whose solve did not converge; otherwise it keeps its own solver run. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._carry_unconverged_side_solve]
- `get_solution` hands `evolution_solved_topologies`, a store shared by the evolution tasks of a stage, to the evolved case's search. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.get_solution]
- `get_solution` converts `ValueError` into `None` with `solution_failure_reason` set. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.get_solution]
- `staged_utilities` (default off) passes `staged_utilities=True` to the PDM sides and the amalgamated network, and a stage-wise task whose parent's network has staged units takes them on too; stage reduction returns a network that holds levels unchanged, as its utility-cost re-solve would undo them. A side that fails with levels held is re-solved without staging, with a warning, and later sides for it (fewest units, variants, heuristic realisations) follow (`_side_args`); a heuristic side none of whose proposals realise with levels held is realised again without them, with a warning. Heuristic realisations with staging get one more, empty, stage at the pinch end (`_with_pinch_end_stage`), and are solved with each level at its target, then, failing that, with `level_band=LOAD_FRACTION_TOLERANCE` (`_realise_side`, `_solved_side`). [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.load_model, _reduce_stages, _side_without_levels_if_failed, _solve_heuristic_pdm]
- Single-process holds: a stage-wise task without `site_processes`, with `level_holds` "fixed" (`HENS_EVOLUTION_LEVEL_HOLDS`; default "none") and with `utility_level_targets` (its own or its parent's), passes the model `held_level_targets` when given (the levels the network it starts from holds; a design that dropped the levels on one pinch side still records every target, and holding it to those it missed made every evolution fail on the review's heat pump level case) and those levels otherwise, as `level_targets` over every stream of the opposite kind (`site_level_targets` with the one process `""`), so the thermal derivative and evolution steps keep each level at the duty the pinch design held. No `stream_groups` are passed, so no match is ruled out [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.load_model].
- Site holds: a stage-wise task with `site_processes` passes the model each process's levels as `level_targets` and each stream's process as `stream_groups` (`_stagewise/level_targets.py:site_level_targets`, after slicing, so a level's slices count together; a stream of no listed process is in group `""`); with `site_level_holds="at_least"` (default `"fixed"`) each target holds its level at or above the duty rather than at it. A relaxed site task with no parent would hold every staged unit on (relaxed binaries are fixed at their warm-start flags), so it is first warm-started from a MILP on the same inputs (HiGHS, isothermal, utility costs, every seed match held in use), and its seed matches are then set back in use. The MILP is given `backend.PLACED_UNITS_TIME_LIMIT` (60 s), or the time limit in the task's own solver options under any solver's key, and a solve stopped there gives its incumbent as the warm start; without HiGHS, or when the MILP fails or has no incumbent, the task runs as built [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.load_model, _build_stage_wise, _placed_staged_units].
- Forbidden matches: every array is a model attribute, so a model built from arrays with the `HENS_FORBIDDEN_MATCHES` masks zeroes `z_feasible` for a forbidden recovery pair in every stage (pinch sides and stage-wise model) and returns 0 from `utility_match_feasibility` for a forbidden heater or cooler, which sets `z_hu_feasible` and `z_cu_feasible` in both. The heuristic proposals and the evolution add moves read those flags [_pinch_design/preprocessing.py] [_stagewise/setup.py] [_base/approach.py:utility_match_feasibility].
- Site balance: a site task (`site_utility_system` set, from `../site/levels.py`) gives its stage-wise model `SiteUtilities` on its (sliced) arrays, so the model closes each level per period and the site pays only for its net utility use; every network it extracts carries the solved balance and the site cost (`_stagewise/site_utilities.py:with_site_cost`). When evolution adopts a candidate the model takes the candidate's solved imports, dumps and letdowns with its duties, so each level still closes [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.load_model, extract_candidate_network] [_stagewise/evolution.py:_adopt_site_balance].
- Level targets: a PDM task with `staged_utilities` records each utility's targeted duty per period by utility name (`utility_level_targets`, slices summed under their parent), a site task (`site_processes`: each process's targeted duty per period by utility name, keyed by its stream-key prefix) records their sums, a task built on a parent with them inherits them, and every network it extracts carries them as `source_metadata["utility_level_targets"]` [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._record_level_targets, extract_candidate_network].
- Utility slices: `load_model` first slices the sliding utilities (`../solver/utility_slices.py`) of a PDM or site task with `staged_utilities` and `utility_slicing` (default on), and gives a task whose parent's arrays are sliced the parent's slices; it replaces `solver_arrays`, slices the decompositions (each slice taking its share of the level target) and copies a heater or cooler restriction to each slice of its utility. Arrays already sliced are kept [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._slice_utilities, _sliced_restriction].
- A `TDM` task's solver options get `backend.TDM_TIME_LIMIT` (300 s) under the solver's time-limit option (`TIME_LIMIT_OPTIONS`) unless the user set one; a solve stopped there passes, unconverged, only with an incumbent, which is verified like any network [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.load_model].
- With `heuristic_network_limit` above 0, a PDM problem runs the heuristic pinch design method (`../heuristic`) instead of the MILP sides and adds no minimum-unit variants: each required side's proposals (up to the limit) are solved for minimum utility cost with only their matches, heaters and coolers allowed and their own stage count (`_realise_side`; a side that fails to build or solve, or stops at its time limit (`backend.HEURISTIC_SIDE_TIME_LIMIT`, 10 s unless the user sets one), is dropped, and the solved side takes the problem's restriction back for amalgamation). A realised side whose network repeats an earlier one (`_side_topology`: its recovery matches, numbered over the stages carrying recovery, the heaters and coolers it uses, and whether it leaves a stage empty, since only a pairing with an empty stage is re-solved at stage reduction) is not paired, as each of its pairings would amalgamate into a repeated network; it is kept as a stand-in, since it may differ in duties, and a pairing that fails is retried with the repeats of either side (`_verified_heuristic_variant`). Side pairs, lowest summed proposal rank first, are amalgamated, stage-reduced and verified like the extra PDM networks; failures and repeated structure signatures are dropped, up to the limit. Past `backend.HEURISTIC_STAGE_TIME_LIMIT` seconds of wall clock from the stage's start, a side stops at its first realised proposal (a side holding its utility levels tries one more proposal, then drops its levels) and the pairing at its first network; the stage keeps the networks found so far and logs a warning. The first network is the problem's case and the rest `pdm_variants`; `heuristic_report` counts proposals, realised sides, repeated sides per side, pairs, failures, repeats and networks, says whether the time limit cut the stage short (`time_limited`), and is logged at INFO; no network raises `ValueError` [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._solve_heuristic_pdm, _realise_side, _verified_heuristic_variant]
- With an integer solver, each solved PDM side is re-solved for the fewest units (recovery matches, heaters and coolers) among those its minimum-utility solve switched on (same superstructure, other binaries fixed at zero, warm-started from that solution, utility cost capped at its minimum, time-limited by `backend.FEWEST_UNITS_TIME_LIMIT`); the re-solve keeps the first solve's convergence, and the original side is kept unless the re-solve is sound with no more units or utility. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._fewest_units_side, _start_from_side]
- With `pdm_network_limits=(at_minimum, one_more)` and an integer solver, a successful PDM solve also fills `pdm_variants`: unless a primary side is unconverged, each required side is re-solved for the fewest units with stage packing and no-good cuts (excluding its minimum-utility topology and each one found), first up to `at_minimum` sides with the fewest units (at least one when `one_more` is positive, so that tier counts from the true minimum, not the primary's count), then, at least one unit more required, up to `one_more` with one more, each from a sound, time-limited solve; side pairs with the fewest total units, then one more, are amalgamated and stripped of empty stages, up to the two limits, and a pair whose network repeats the main network or an earlier variant is dropped. Each variant is a shallow copy of the problem with its own sides and case. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._solve_pdm_variants]
- Stage reduction keeps stages whose share of peak recovery meets a threshold (0.1, 5 and 8 percent for at most 3, 5 and 8 stages, 10 percent for more; PDM and TDM solves only), or, when no stage meets it, every stage with recovery, and returns the original case unless the reduced model solves, verifies, and has a total annual cost and each period's hot and cold utility no greater than the original's (within `tol` or 1e-6 relative). The reduced model is a relaxed Pyomo `StageWiseModel`, with the case's `solver_options`, solved with HiGHS when it is linear (a PDM reduction) and with Ipopt otherwise, or when highspy is missing; a model moved from HiGHS to Ipopt keeps only the case's time limit (under any supported solver's key, `backend.TIME_LIMIT_OPTIONS`, or Ipopt's `max_wall_time` or `max_cpu_time`), as Ipopt's `max_wall_time`, since the other options do not apply there [problem.py:_ipopt_time_limit]. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._utilisation_threshold] [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.remove_unused_stages]

## Side Effects
- Every model construction creates a native Pyomo model and checks its solver, and `optimise` runs the solver (see `_base`). [base.py:BaseHeatExchangerNetworkModel.setup_model]
- `get_solution` removes the run directories of the above, below, unreduced and final cases in a `finally` block, except the amalgamated model when amalgamation raises. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.get_solution]
- `problem.py` sets `args`, `above`, `below`, `case` and `solution_failure_reason` on the dataclass instance. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.load_model]
- `remove_unused_stages` builds and solves a reduced `StageWiseModel` with HiGHS, or with `ipopt-pyomo` when HiGHS is unavailable or the reduced model is not linear. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem.remove_unused_stages]
- `PinchDecompModel` copies the original stream temperatures to `*_OG` attributes. [pinch_decomposition.py:PinchDecompModel.get_model_parameters_from_solver_arrays]
- Stage packing adds binaries and equations to the model it receives. [stage_packing.py:add_recovery_stage_packing_constraints]
- `problem.py` imports extraction at module level and `PinchDecompModel` lazily; `_stagewise/verification.py` lazily imports extraction and reporting. [problem.py:InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._model_factory]
