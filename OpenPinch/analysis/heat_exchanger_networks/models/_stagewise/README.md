# StageWise Model Helpers

## Overview
Private helper modules for `StageWiseModel` (`../stagewise.py`), which delegates each lifecycle step to one module here: preprocessing, superstructure equations, staged utilities, objectives, warm start, post-processing, verification and topology evolution. They rely on `../_base` for shared equations, areas and costs, and `../_pinch_design/postprocess.py` reuses `verification._value`.

## Interface
- `set_preprocessing` (`setup.py`): stage counts, total duties, U values, `Q_max`, big-M and feasibility flags.
- `set_stage_wise_superstructure`, `set_dqda_equations` (`equations.py`): variables, balances, approach constraints, isothermal or non-isothermal mixing, minimum dQ/dA. Parent also calls `_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure`.
- `create_staged_utility_grids`, `set_staged_utility_equations`, `post_process_staged_utilities`, `with_staged_duties`, `staged_terminal_differences`, `set_staged_initial_values`, `staged_units_on`, `staged_heater_duty`, `staged_cooler_duty`, `staged_utility_loads`, `interior_boundaries` (`staged_utilities.py`): heaters and coolers between stages, offered when the model's `staged_utilities` is on: duties `Q_hs_by_period[n][j][u][k]` and `Q_cs_by_period[n][i][u][k]`, binaries `z_hs`, `z_cs`, flags `z_hs_allowed`, `z_cs_allowed`, and mid temperatures `T_c_mid_by_period[n][j][k]`, `T_h_mid_by_period[n][i][k]`.
- `set_obj` (`objectives.py`): objective for each supported goal, including total-cost forms.
- `UtilityLevelTarget`, `set_level_target_equations`, `LEVEL_BAND` (`level_targets.py`): a level's duty per period over a group of process streams (cold streams for a hot utility, hot streams for a cold one), over one or more utility indices (a sliced level's slices); the model's `level_targets` holds each within a relative `LEVEL_BAND` (1e-7), or an `at_least` target at or above its duty less the band, stream-end and staged units together, and a target no exchanger can serve (one above zero, for `at_least`) raises `ValueError`.
- `SiteUtilities`, `site_costs`, `site_cost_value`, `site_balance`, `balance_misses`, `with_site_cost`, `BALANCE_TOLERANCE` (`site_utilities.py`): a site model's levels and the level of each of its utilities (a slice serves its parent's); the period's site balance rows and its import and dump-plus-letdown costs, added once per period and model; the solved cost of a side; the solved balance (`UtilitySystemBalance`); levels whose solved balance misses closing by more than `BALANCE_TOLERANCE` (1e-5, relative to the largest flow or of 1 kW); and a network with `site_capital_cost`, `site_operating_cost`, `site_total_annual_cost` in its summary metrics and the balance in `source_metadata["site_utility_balance"]`.
- `set_initial_values_for_variables` (`warm_start.py`): copies a solved model's values into another (staged units through `staged_utilities.set_staged_initial_values`: each offered unit takes the parent's binary, duties and mid temperatures, or is switched off with no duty when the parent did not offer it), then switches on the relaxed model's idle-safe heaters and coolers (`_base/approach.py:switch_on_idle_safe_utilities`).
- `get_post_process`, `get_lowest_benefit_HX`, `get_lowest_benefit_HX_candidates`, `get_max_benefit_HX`, `get_max_benefit_HX_candidates` (`postprocess.py`): result extraction, dQ/dA, alpha, and ranked remove or add candidates. Parent also calls `_get_multiperiod_post_process`.
- `verify` (`verification.py`): temperature, cost, area and network-feasibility checks; returns `(ok, reasons)`. `_value` (from `_base.execution`) reads plain numbers, `[value]` lists and Pyomo components.
- `get_net_benefit_evolution`, `get_n_minus_one_evolution`, `get_n_plus_one_evolution` (`evolution.py`): topology evolution. Parent also calls `_active_binary_value`.
- `reporting_improvements(callback)` (`evolution.py`): context manager; searches run inside it pass each candidate that improves on the search's best to `callback`.
- Subdirectories: none.

## Invariants
- Hot streams enter at stage boundary 0 (fixed parameter) and cold streams at boundary `S` (fixed parameter); with isothermal mixing (PDM, TDM) `theta_1` pairs `T_h[k]` with `T_c[k]` and `theta_2` pairs `T_h[k+1]` with `T_c[k+1]`; with non-isothermal mixing (ESM) `theta_1 = T_h[k] - T_c_out_y` and `theta_2 = T_h_out_x - T_c[k+1]` using the per-match branch outlets. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_isothermal_approach_equations]
- A disallowed match's `Q_r`, `theta_1`, `theta_2`, `X` and `Y` slots are plain numbers (0, or the match's minimum approach for `theta_1` and `theta_2`), not Pyomo components. Its `T_h_out_x` and `T_c_out_y` slots stay parameters: warm start writes the stage outlet temperatures there, and an evolution move that adds the match starts from them. Warm starts, single-period and multiperiod, leave a disallowed match's binary, and a disallowed heater's or cooler's (`z_hu`, `z_cu`), at 0 whatever the source had. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_non_isothermal_equations] [warm_start.py:_set_single_period_initial_values]
- `M_ij_period` and `Q_max_period` are nested lists of Python floats, not NumPy arrays: they multiply binaries in the big-M constraints, and a NumPy scalar in front of a Pyomo term goes through NumPy's ufunc dispatch first. `M_ij` and `Q_max`, their per-period maxima, stay NumPy arrays. [setup.py:set_preprocessing]
- With `values_only=True` the model creates the same variables and parameters, in the same order and with the same values and bounds, but no constraints or objective: the isothermal and utility approach builders and the TDM minimum dQ/dA restriction, which add constraints only, are skipped, the balance, binary and non-isothermal constraint expressions are not built, and the objective is not added. A segmented stream's piecewise mappings, which create their coordinate variables together with their constraints, are still built and their constraints dropped. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [../stagewise.py:StageWiseModel.setup]
- Over several periods each match's theta is only bounded by its actual approach (one shared area, with bypass in a period that does not set it), so `verify` requires each period's approach to be at least its theta (within tolerance) rather than equal to it; a single-period model still requires equality. [verification.py:_check_state_temperatures]
- With shared areas, `verify` recomputes the area costs from the matches still allowed (a match evolution removed keeps its warm-start area, which the model no longer costs) and counts an area the solver left a hair below zero as none, rather than raising it to a fractional exponent. [verification.py:_check_shared_area_costs, _used_area]
- Without per-period area variables, `verify` checks each stage's recovery area cost against the Chen-mean areas with the model's own smoothing (`+ 1e-3` m2 before the cost exponent, `objectives.py`), so a tiny match, whose cost is mostly that term, is not rejected for it. [verification.py:_check_area_costs]
- With `stream_groups` (a label per hot and per cold stream), a recovery match between streams of different groups is not feasible (`z_feasible` 0 at every stage), so it is never in the superstructure and no add move proposes it. [setup.py:set_preprocessing] [postprocess.py:get_max_benefit_HX_candidates]
- Staged heaters and coolers sit only at interior boundaries `k` (1 to S-1), at most one per stream and boundary when binaries are integer. A cold stream leaves stage `k` at `T_c_mid[k]` and enters stage `k-1` at `T_c[k]`, `f * (T_c[k] - T_c_mid[k])` being its staged heater duty; a hot stream leaves stage `k-1` at `T_h_mid[k]` and enters stage `k` at `T_h[k]`. Stage energy balances and the isothermal approaches use the mid temperature as the stage outlet. A staged unit's utility inlet faces the stream's outlet from it and its utility outlet the stream's inlet, each at the stream-end unit's approach, relaxed by big-M when its binary is off. Where no staged unit is offered (`staged_utilities` off, a segmented stream or utility (read from the solver arrays), a stream that runs in no period, the wrong side of the pinch on a pinch side, or a utility whose inlet cannot clear the stream's supply by its approach) the mid temperature is the boundary temperature itself, so the model is unchanged. Post-processing replaces each offered staged binary with `[flag]` (used in some period), counts used staged units in `n_hu_units` and `n_cu_units`, adds their duties to the utility totals and costs, and sizes them (`area_hs`, `area_cs`: the largest over periods, at the exact log-mean), which `_base/costing.py` adds to the utility area cost; with staging off it sets no units and empty grids. Every goal counts staged duties with the stream-end ones. The total-cost goals give each staged unit one area variable (`area_hs_shared`, `area_cs_shared`, keyed `(stream, utility, boundary)`) at least each period's Chen-mean requirement, costed with the utility area law, and "total cost" adds the utility unit cost of each staged binary; `verify` reads the mid temperatures as stage outlets and checks the staged duties, costs and areas. [staged_utilities.py] [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [objectives.py:_staged_area_cost, _staged_unit_cost] [verification.py:_check_state_temperatures, _staged_areas_cover_duties]
- The theta grids look up each recovery approach temperature once per (period, hot, cold) pair, not per stage. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure]
- An allowed match that cannot carry duty in a period (its duty bound there, `Q_max_period`, at most `tol`, as when its supply temperatures are closer than its approach) has constant `theta_1` and `theta_2` (its approach) and no approach or minimum dQ/dA constraints in that period, so its bounds there cannot cross and a match chosen in another period does not bind it; the warm start leaves those constants alone [equations.py:_runs] [equations.py:set_dqda_equations] [warm_start.py:_set_multiperiod_initial_values].
- Topology (`z`, `z_hu`, `z_cu`) is shared across periods; flows, temperatures and approaches are per period. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure]
- Supported goals: "hot utility", "cold utility", "total utility", "utility costs", "heat recovery", "dQ/dA obj", "total cost", "variable total cost"; others raise `ValueError`. [objectives.py:set_obj]
- Solver area terms use Chen's LMTD approximation, with each terminal difference passed through a smooth max with zero (smoothing 1e-4 K) so an unused heater or cooler cannot make the base negative; multiperiod total cost uses one shared area per unit bounded below by each period's requirement. [objectives.py:_chen_mean] [objectives.py:_set_multiperiod_total_cost_objective]
- `verify` checks cost and area consistency only for the two total-cost goals; once those pass it also runs the reporting-layer network feasibility check. [verification.py:verify]
- Evolution candidates are usable only if solved, converged (`solver_run.converged`) and `verify()` passes; the best is adopted only if it verifies and, when the starting solve converged, its TAC is below the original. When the starting solve did not converge its TAC is not a benchmark: the best usable candidate is adopted, and the workflow's final ranking compares it with the seed network on verified costs. [evolution.py:_is_usable_evolution_candidate] [evolution.py:_benchmark_model] [evolution.py:_update_with_best_model]
- With more than one add or remove branch, or a patience limit, evolution keeps at most `beam_width` branches ranked by TAC then topology signature, solves each topology once, and orders parallel results by (branch, kind, rank) so serial and parallel runs agree. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_solve_evolution_candidates]
- With one add branch, one remove branch and no patience, the original single-path search runs. It solves each topology once: a move back to a topology already solved in the search reuses that child, warm start and all, rather than re-solving it. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_get_source_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- Both searches pass a candidate to the `reporting_improvements` callback each time it becomes the search's best (strictly lower TAC, or the first usable one when the start did not converge), in the order the search accepts them, so serial and parallel candidate solves report the same sequence. [evolution.py:_report_improvement] [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_get_source_net_benefit_evolution]
- Given a `solved_topologies` store, a search shares its solved children with every other search given the same store whose model inputs are equal (framework, stages, `dTmin`, `min_dqda`, objective, mixing model, solver and its options, the content of the solver arrays, the staged units switched on (`staged_utilities.staged_units_on`), the level targets and the stream groups): a topology one search solved is reused by the next, on both search paths, rather than solved again. A reused child keeps the warm start of the search that first solved it. Without a store each search keeps its own. [evolution.py:_shared_solved_topologies] [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- Evolution children are relaxed (`integers=False`, `tol=1e-3`), use the root model's solver and take its parent's `staged_utilities`, `level_targets` and `stream_groups`; the warm start fixes their staged units at the parent's flags, so moves change recovery matches only. [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- Post-processing reads each solved value it needs once: an inactive match's log-mean is 0 without reading its approaches, the stream temperatures are read once per stream and boundary, and the segment dQ/dA is computed only for matches with a segmented stream. [postprocess.py:_get_multiperiod_post_process]
- Remove ranking is ascending net benefit over active matches; add ranking is descending alpha-weighted dQ/dA over inactive, feasible matches with positive value; ties break by index order. [postprocess.py:get_lowest_benefit_HX_candidates] [postprocess.py:get_max_benefit_HX_candidates]
- A match's dQ/dA (`dqda`) is each period's dQ/dA (from that period's approaches, stream temperatures, coefficient and, for a segmented stream, segment areas) averaged by the period weights; with one period it is that period's. [postprocess.py:period_weighted_dqda]
- Evolution of a model whose own solve failed returns the model unevolved and sets `evolution_skipped` on it. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution]
- Warm start uses the last source period when the source has fewer periods. [warm_start.py:_set_multiperiod_initial_values]

- A model with `site_utilities` costs utilities through the site balance: each period's hot cost is the site's imports and its cold cost the dumps and letdowns, for every goal that prices utilities (`utility costs`, `total cost`, `variable total cost`), so the site pays only for its net utility use; heaters and coolers carry no price of their own. Post-processing and `verify` read the solved balance for the utility cost, `verify` adds each level that does not close, and evolution children take the parent's `site_utilities` (also in the shared-children key) [objectives.py:_utilities_cost_expression] [../stagewise.py:StageWiseModel._utilities_cost_value] [verification.py:verify] [evolution.py].

## Side Effects
- Evolution builds and solves child `StageWiseModel` instances (native Pyomo models), in a `ThreadPoolExecutor` when `max_parallel` exceeds one and `supported_workers` allows it. [evolution.py:_solve_evolution_candidates]
- Each evolution candidate is warm-started from its parent's solution, and the add or remove move is then applied over those values. [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- `get_net_benefit_evolution` ends by calling `owner.cleanup()` on both search paths (only the early return when `owner.mSuccess != 1` skips it), and `_update_with_best_model` first turns the owner's plain-number slots of each match the adopted network adds into parameters holding the same numbers, then overwrites the owner's variables, `z_allowed`, alpha, cost attributes, shared areas (recovery, stream-end and staged units alike) and `solver_run`, then re-runs post-processing. The solver's own cost lists (`hu_cost_solver_by_period`, `cu_cost_solver_by_period`, `recovery_area_cost_filtered`) are adopted as solved floats, not expressions on the candidate's model. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_update_with_best_model] [evolution.py:_open_added_match_slots]
- `verify` lazily imports and runs `extract_heat_exchanger_network` and `verify_network_feasibility`. [verification.py:_check_network_feasibility]
- Post-processing leaves the post-optimisation alpha solve (`get_alpha_values`) and alpha-weighted dQ/dA (`alpha_dqda`) pending; the first remove or add ranking of the model solves them, once, and `_update_with_best_model` solves them for the adopted candidate before copying its alpha. [postprocess.py:_solve_ranking_alpha] [evolution.py:_update_with_best_model]
- Evolution and verification log mostly at debug level; exceptions in a candidate solve are caught and logged. [evolution.py:_solve_evolution_candidate]
