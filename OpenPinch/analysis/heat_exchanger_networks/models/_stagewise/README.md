# StageWise Model Helpers

## Overview
Private helper modules for `StageWiseModel` (`../stagewise.py`), which delegates each lifecycle step to one module here: preprocessing, superstructure equations, staged utilities, objectives, warm start, post-processing, verification and topology evolution. They rely on `../_base` for shared equations, areas and costs, and `../_pinch_design/postprocess.py` reuses `verification._value`.

## Interface
- `set_preprocessing` (`setup.py`): stage counts, total duties, U values, `Q_max`, big-M and feasibility flags.
- `set_stage_wise_superstructure`, `set_dqda_equations` (`equations.py`): variables, balances, approach constraints, isothermal or non-isothermal mixing, minimum dQ/dA. Parent also calls `_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure`.
- `create_staged_utility_grids`, `set_staged_utility_equations`, `post_process_staged_utilities`, `with_staged_duties`, `staged_terminal_differences`, `set_staged_initial_values`, `staged_heater_duty`, `staged_cooler_duty`, `staged_utility_loads`, `interior_boundaries` (`staged_utilities.py`): heaters and coolers between stages, offered when the model's `staged_utilities` is on: duties `Q_hs_by_period[n][j][u][k]` and `Q_cs_by_period[n][i][u][k]`, binaries `z_hs`, `z_cs`, flags `z_hs_allowed`, `z_cs_allowed`, and mid temperatures `T_c_mid_by_period[n][j][k]`, `T_h_mid_by_period[n][i][k]`.
- `set_obj` (`objectives.py`): objective for each supported goal, including total-cost forms.
- `set_initial_values_for_variables` (`warm_start.py`): copies a solved model's values into another (staged units through `staged_utilities.set_staged_initial_values`: each offered unit takes the parent's binary, duties and mid temperatures, or is switched off with no duty when the parent did not offer it), then switches on the relaxed model's idle-safe heaters and coolers (`_base/approach.py:switch_on_idle_safe_utilities`).
- `get_post_process`, `get_lowest_benefit_HX`, `get_lowest_benefit_HX_candidates`, `get_max_benefit_HX`, `get_max_benefit_HX_candidates` (`postprocess.py`): result extraction, dQ/dA, alpha, and ranked remove or add candidates. Parent also calls `_get_multiperiod_post_process`.
- `verify` (`verification.py`): temperature, cost, area and network-feasibility checks; returns `(ok, reasons)`. `_value` (from `_base.execution`) reads plain numbers, `[value]` lists and Pyomo components.
- `get_net_benefit_evolution`, `get_n_minus_one_evolution`, `get_n_plus_one_evolution` (`evolution.py`): topology evolution. Parent also calls `_active_binary_value`.
- `reporting_improvements(callback)` (`evolution.py`): context manager; searches run inside it pass each candidate that improves on the search's best to `callback`.
- Subdirectories: none.

## Invariants
- Hot streams enter at stage boundary 0 (fixed parameter) and cold streams at boundary `S` (fixed parameter); with isothermal mixing (PDM, TDM) `theta_1` pairs `T_h[k]` with `T_c[k]` and `theta_2` pairs `T_h[k+1]` with `T_c[k+1]`; with non-isothermal mixing (ESM) `theta_1 = T_h[k] - T_c_out_y` and `theta_2 = T_h_out_x - T_c[k+1]` using the per-match branch outlets. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_isothermal_approach_equations]
- A disallowed match's `Q_r`, `theta_1`, `theta_2`, `X` and `Y` slots are plain numbers (0, or the match's minimum approach for `theta_1` and `theta_2`), not Pyomo components. Its `T_h_out_x` and `T_c_out_y` slots stay parameters: warm start writes the stage outlet temperatures there, and an evolution move that adds the match starts from them. Warm starts, single-period and multiperiod, leave a disallowed match's binary at 0 whatever the source had. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_non_isothermal_equations] [warm_start.py:_set_single_period_initial_values]
- `M_ij_period` and `Q_max_period` are nested lists of Python floats, not NumPy arrays: they multiply binaries in the big-M constraints, and a NumPy scalar in front of a Pyomo term goes through NumPy's ufunc dispatch first. `M_ij` and `Q_max`, their per-period maxima, stay NumPy arrays. [setup.py:set_preprocessing]
- With `values_only=True` the model creates the same variables and parameters, in the same order and with the same values and bounds, but no constraints or objective: the isothermal and utility approach builders and the TDM minimum dQ/dA restriction, which add constraints only, are skipped, the balance, binary and non-isothermal constraint expressions are not built, and the objective is not added. A segmented stream's piecewise mappings, which create their coordinate variables together with their constraints, are still built and their constraints dropped. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [../stagewise.py:StageWiseModel.setup]
- Staged heaters and coolers sit only at interior boundaries `k` (1 to S-1), at most one per stream and boundary when binaries are integer. A cold stream leaves stage `k` at `T_c_mid[k]` and enters stage `k-1` at `T_c[k]`, `f * (T_c[k] - T_c_mid[k])` being its staged heater duty; a hot stream leaves stage `k-1` at `T_h_mid[k]` and enters stage `k` at `T_h[k]`. Stage energy balances and the isothermal approaches use the mid temperature as the stage outlet. A staged unit's utility inlet faces the stream's outlet from it and its utility outlet the stream's inlet, each at the stream-end unit's approach, relaxed by big-M when its binary is off. Where no staged unit is offered (`staged_utilities` off, a segmented stream or utility, or a utility whose inlet cannot clear the stream's supply by its approach) the mid temperature is the boundary temperature itself, so the model is unchanged. Post-processing counts used staged units in `n_hu_units` and `n_cu_units`, adds their duties to the utility totals and costs, and sizes them (`area_hs`, `area_cs`: the largest over periods, at the exact log-mean), which `_base/costing.py` adds to the utility area cost; with staging off it sets no units and empty grids. Every goal counts staged duties with the stream-end ones. The total-cost goals give each staged unit one area variable (`area_hs_shared`, `area_cs_shared`, keyed `(stream, utility, boundary)`) at least each period's Chen-mean requirement, costed with the utility area law, and "total cost" adds the utility unit cost of each staged binary; `verify` reads the mid temperatures as stage outlets and checks the staged duties, costs and areas. [staged_utilities.py] [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure] [objectives.py:_staged_area_cost, _staged_unit_cost] [verification.py:_check_state_temperatures, _staged_areas_cover_duties]
- The theta grids look up each recovery approach temperature once per (period, hot, cold) pair, not per stage. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure]
- Topology (`z`, `z_hu`, `z_cu`) is shared across periods; flows, temperatures and approaches are per period. [equations.py:_set_multiperiod_stage_wise_superstructure]
- Supported goals: "hot utility", "cold utility", "total utility", "utility costs", "heat recovery", "dQ/dA obj", "total cost", "variable total cost"; others raise `ValueError`. [objectives.py:set_obj]
- Solver area terms use Chen's LMTD approximation, with each terminal difference passed through a smooth max with zero (smoothing 1e-4 K) so an unused heater or cooler cannot make the base negative; multiperiod total cost uses one shared area per unit bounded below by each period's requirement. [objectives.py:_chen_mean] [objectives.py:_set_multiperiod_total_cost_objective]
- `verify` checks cost and area consistency only for the two total-cost goals; once those pass it also runs the reporting-layer network feasibility check. [verification.py:verify]
- Evolution candidates are usable only if solved, converged (`solver_run.converged`) and `verify()` passes; the best is adopted only if it verifies and, when the starting solve converged, its TAC is below the original. When the starting solve did not converge its TAC is not a benchmark: the best usable candidate is adopted, and the workflow's final ranking compares it with the seed network on verified costs. [evolution.py:_is_usable_evolution_candidate] [evolution.py:_benchmark_model] [evolution.py:_update_with_best_model]
- With more than one add or remove branch, or a patience limit, evolution keeps at most `beam_width` branches ranked by TAC then topology signature, solves each topology once, and orders parallel results by (branch, kind, rank) so serial and parallel runs agree. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_solve_evolution_candidates]
- With one add branch, one remove branch and no patience, the original single-path search runs. It solves each topology once: a move back to a topology already solved in the search reuses that child, warm start and all, rather than re-solving it. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_get_source_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- Both searches pass a candidate to the `reporting_improvements` callback each time it becomes the search's best (strictly lower TAC, or the first usable one when the start did not converge), in the order the search accepts them, so serial and parallel candidate solves report the same sequence. [evolution.py:_report_improvement] [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_get_source_net_benefit_evolution]
- Given a `solved_topologies` store, a search shares its solved children with every other search given the same store whose model inputs are equal (framework, stages, `dTmin`, `min_dqda`, objective, mixing model, solver and its options, and the content of the solver arrays): a topology one search solved is reused by the next, on both search paths, rather than solved again. A reused child keeps the warm start of the search that first solved it. Without a store each search keeps its own. [evolution.py:_shared_solved_topologies] [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- Evolution children are relaxed (`integers=False`, `tol=1e-3`) and use the root model's solver. [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- Post-processing reads each solved value it needs once: an inactive match's log-mean is 0 without reading its approaches, the stream temperatures are read once per stream and boundary, and the segment dQ/dA is computed only for matches with a segmented stream. [postprocess.py:_get_multiperiod_post_process]
- Remove ranking is ascending net benefit over active matches; add ranking is descending alpha-weighted dQ/dA over inactive, feasible matches with positive value; ties break by index order. [postprocess.py:get_lowest_benefit_HX_candidates] [postprocess.py:get_max_benefit_HX_candidates]
- Warm start uses the last source period when the source has fewer periods. [warm_start.py:_set_multiperiod_initial_values]

## Side Effects
- Evolution builds and solves child `StageWiseModel` instances (native Pyomo models), in a `ThreadPoolExecutor` when `max_parallel` exceeds one and `supported_workers` allows it. [evolution.py:_solve_evolution_candidates]
- Each evolution candidate is warm-started from its parent's solution, and the add or remove move is then applied over those values. [evolution.py:_build_and_solve_evolution]
- `get_net_benefit_evolution` ends by calling `owner.cleanup()` on both search paths (only the early return when `owner.mSuccess != 1` skips it), and `_update_with_best_model` first turns the owner's plain-number slots of each match the adopted network adds into parameters holding the same numbers, then overwrites the owner's variables, `z_allowed`, alpha, cost attributes and `solver_run`, then re-runs post-processing. The solver's own cost lists (`hu_cost_solver_by_period`, `cu_cost_solver_by_period`, `recovery_area_cost_filtered`) are adopted as solved floats, not expressions on the candidate's model. [evolution.py:get_net_benefit_evolution] [evolution.py:_update_with_best_model] [evolution.py:_open_added_match_slots]
- `verify` lazily imports and runs `extract_heat_exchanger_network` and `verify_network_feasibility`. [verification.py:_check_network_feasibility]
- Post-processing leaves the post-optimisation alpha solve (`get_alpha_values`) and alpha-weighted dQ/dA (`alpha_dqda`) pending; the first remove or add ranking of the model solves them, once, and `_update_with_best_model` solves them for the adopted candidate before copying its alpha. [postprocess.py:_solve_ranking_alpha] [evolution.py:_update_with_best_model]
- Evolution and verification log mostly at debug level; exceptions in a candidate solve are caught and logged. [evolution.py:_solve_evolution_candidate]
