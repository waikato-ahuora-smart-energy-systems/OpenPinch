# HEN Base-Model Helpers

## Overview
Private helper modules that hold the equation, post-processing and solver-execution logic shared by `PinchDecompModel` and `StageWiseModel`. `../base.py` (`BaseHeatExchangerNetworkModel`) delegates to these modules, and `../_stagewise` and `../_pinch_design` import them directly. Every function takes the owning model as its first argument and reads or writes attributes on it; nothing here defines a class.

## Interface
The parent imports `alpha`, `approach`, `execution`, `parameters` and `piecewise`; `area`, `costing` and `superstructure` are used directly by `_stagewise` and `_pinch_design`.
- `get_alpha_values`, `alpha_value` (`alpha.py`): post-optimisation alpha flow-on solve and neutral-alpha lookup.
- `utility_minimum_approach_by_period`, `utility_match_feasibility`, `set_match_restrictions` (`approach.py`): least heater and cooler approach by period, reachable (stream, utility) flags, and the `z_allowed`, `z_hu_allowed`, `z_cu_allowed` restriction arrays. `switch_on_idle_safe_utilities` switches on, in a relaxed model, each allowed heater or cooler that can sit idle within its approach, so evolution can add one its parent lacked. Underscore helpers used by siblings: `_set_minimum_approach_temperatures`, `_recovery_approach_temperature`, `_utility_solved_outlet_temperature`, `_set_multiperiod_utility_approach_equations`, `_weighted_state_average`.
- `solved_utility_duties`, `used_utility_matches`, `recovered_utility_price`, `recovered_value`, `set_utility_lmtd_and_areas` (`area.py`): solved heater and cooler duties, used-unit flags, recovered-heat price (per period), a match's recovered utility cost averaged over periods by their weights (the removal ranking's benefit), and utility LMTD and area by period. Used by siblings: `_post_process_lmtd`, `_apply_segment_recovery_areas`, `_segment_exact_dqda`.
- `set_utility_slice_coupling` (`utility_slices.py`): holds a sliced utility's neighbouring slices to their shares of its range, `Q[s] * share[t] == Q[t] * share[s]` per period, stream-end and staged duties together (the slices run in series). The PDM and stage-wise equations add it with the staged-utility equations; it adds nothing without slices.
- `set_period_totals_and_costs` (`costing.py`): per-period duty totals, operating cost and capital cost; the utility area cost also covers a stage-wise model's staged heaters and coolers (`area_hs`, `area_cs`) when it has them.
- `optimise`, `cleanup_model`, `setup_model`, `output_to_cmd_line` (`execution.py`): solve loop, run directory removal, native Pyomo model and solver creation, diagnostics. Used by siblings: `_solver_value`, `_set_value`, `_assign_value`.
- `set_blank_input_parameters`, `get_model_parameters_from_solver_arrays` (`parameters.py`): blank parameter arrays, then population from `PreparedSolverArrays` with period validation.
- `create_utility_duty_grids`, `create_match_binaries` (`superstructure.py`): utility duty variables and recovery or utility match binaries.
- `install_interior_cuts`, `replay_interior_cuts` (`interior_approach.py`): the approach inside a match on a segmented stream, as lazy cuts on a model with isothermal stage temperatures (the pinch design side and the isothermal stage-wise model, with or without integers), and the replay of one model's cuts onto a model of the same superstructure.
- `piecewise.py`: underscore-only helpers for segmented streams and utilities (`_set_piecewise_stage_heat_coordinates`, `_set_piecewise_match_outlet_equations`, `_utility_cost_expression`, `_utility_cost_value`, `_parent_profile_duty`, `_recovery_heat_upper_bound`, `_solver_parent_is_segmented`, `_update_piecewise_active_segments`).
- Subdirectories: none.

## Invariants
- `period_ids` and `period_weights` must be present, the weights finite with a positive sum and one per period; the stream and utility `*_period` input arrays are non-empty, 2-D, with one row per period. [parameters.py:_normalise_state_arrays]
- The unsuffixed arrays (`T_h_in`, `f_h`, `dT_r` and similar) are copies of period 0. [parameters.py:_normalise_state_arrays] [approach.py:_set_minimum_approach_temperatures]
- Grid orientation: recovery `[hot][cold][stage]`, heaters `[period][cold][utility]`, coolers `[period][hot][utility]`; each (stream, utility) pair is its own unit. [superstructure.py:create_utility_duty_grids] [approach.py:utility_minimum_approach_by_period]
- A disallowed recovery match's binary is the plain number `0.0`; a disallowed heater's or cooler's binary is a fixed `Param` at 0. [superstructure.py:create_match_binaries]
- With `integers` false the match binaries are `Param`, so a heater or cooler whose fixed end misses its approach is forced to zero duty. [approach.py:_set_terminal_approach_equations]
- Alpha and the gammas are solved from the solved model as one sparse linear system, assembled with whole-grid array operations and storing no zero coefficients; stream duty totals are summed with Python's compensated `sum`, so the duty fractions match it to the last place; a singular system, a residual above 1e-8 or a value outside [-1, 1] returns `None`, is not cached, and ranking falls back to alpha 1.0. [alpha.py:get_alpha_values, _solve_flow_on_system] [alpha.py:alpha_value]
- A crossed match (non-positive terminal difference) has LMTD 0 and so zero area. [area.py:_post_process_lmtd]
- Design area of every unit is the maximum over periods; period totals are weighted by `period_weights`. [area.py:set_utility_lmtd_and_areas] [costing.py:_weighted_numeric_average]
- Capital cost is `unit_cost * n_units` plus power-law area terms for recovery, heaters and coolers. [costing.py:set_period_totals_and_costs]
- With a solver in `backend.INTEGER_SOLVERS` (`couenne`, `highs`, `scip`, `mindtpy`) segment temperature and price profiles use interval binaries; with the others they use relaxed selectors that the solve loop updates. Approach-contribution profiles always use relaxed selectors. [piecewise.py:_set_piecewise_stage_heat_coordinates, _utility_cost_expression]
- After a solve whose piecewise segments are stable, a model with `_add_lazy_cuts` (a callable) checks the solution; when it returns true it has added constraints and the model is solved again, within the same eight attempts. The pinch design side and the isothermal stage-wise model use it for the approach inside matches on segmented streams. If the attempts run out while it is still adding cuts, the solve fails. [execution.py:_solve_and_post_process]
- With a segmented stream, each solution is checked at the segment boundaries strictly inside every match with duty, between the match's inlets and outlets (`_branch_ends`: the stage's temperatures, or with `non_isothermal_model` the match's branch outlets `T_h_out_x_by_period` and `T_c_out_y_by_period`) (the partner's temperature is the one at the same fraction of its own stage heat, counter-current), against the pair approach (`pair_minimum_approach`) of the two segments' own contributions, the boundary's the stricter of its two adjacent segments. Where one falls short (beyond 1e-6 K) the pair gets, in every stage it may use, a linear cut for that boundary: with the boundary inside the hot branch, the boundary less the cold branch outlet at least the boundary's contribution with the partner's largest segment contribution (a cold boundary likewise against the hot branch outlet), switched by the match binary and two binaries for which side of the boundary each branch end lies. A model without integers (network evolution, whose topology is fixed) gets the cut in the crossing stage alone, switched by the match binary only; before each check, a cut whose boundary the solved branch no longer spans and that still binds (within 1e-6 K) is released and the model solved again, and a released cut crossed again is put back for good, so the solve does not cycle. The cut is conservative, so a cut pair may need a stage boundary at the kink. A model with no crossing keeps its solution. Cuts are kept in order (`_interior_cuts`) so `replay_interior_cuts` lines up a second model's variables. [interior_approach.py:install_interior_cuts, _add_interior_approach_cuts, _release_cuts_off_their_boundary, _cut_pair, _cut, replay_interior_cuts]
- Piecewise solves repeat up to eight times until active segments stop changing; failure to stabilise, or a negative objective (beyond `tol`) on a model without `site_utilities`, marks the solve failed; a model that costs a site utility balance may have a negative objective. [execution.py:_solve_and_post_process]
- `_set_value` clamps to variable bounds; a parameter (a fixed `PyomoModel` variable) takes the value unclamped and stays fixed, without Pyomo's domain and bound check. [execution.py:_set_value]
- A plain-number slot (a disallowed match's) keeps its number: `_set_value` and `_assign_value` accept that number or `None` and raise `ValueError` for any other value. [execution.py:_check_constant]
- `_assign_value` writes a value unclamped and unvalidated onto a Pyomo variable or parameter, leaving a parameter fixed; warm starts, amalgamation, evolution moves and stage reduction write values only through it or `_set_value`. [execution.py:_assign_value]
- The solve loop calls `solve_pyomo_model` and judges success, a negative objective and the logged objective from the `SolverRun` status and objective value. [execution.py:_solve_and_post_process]
- A relaxed match (a fixed parameter) whose fixed end misses its approach forces the unit's duty to zero. [approach.py:_set_terminal_approach_equations, _is_parameter]

## Side Effects
- Checks the Pyomo solver (binary first, then Pyomo) and creates a native `PyomoModel`. [execution.py:setup_model]
- `optimise` always removes the model's run directory afterwards; `cleanup_model` is safe to repeat. [execution.py:optimise] [execution.py:cleanup_model]
- A values-only model (`values_only` true) gets a `PyomoModel` without constraints, and `optimise` raises `RuntimeError` for it rather than solving a model with no constraints, still removing its run directory. [execution.py:setup_model] [execution.py:optimise]
- A solve that stopped before converging (`solver_run.converged` false) keeps `mSuccess` and its values, but is logged as a warning (`[Unconverged]`) rather than `[Success]`. [execution.py:_solve_and_post_process]
- A failed solve sets `mSuccess` to 0 and is logged at ERROR, or at the model's `failure_log_level` when a caller that expects failures (heuristic proposals, the minimum-unit search) sets one. A passing status with no objective value is a failure (`solver returned no objective value`). [execution.py:_solve_and_post_process]
- `get_alpha_values` writes the flow-fraction grids onto the model as NumPy arrays (`P_h`, `P_c`, `beta_h`, `beta_c` over (hot, cold, stage); `z_j` over (hot, stage); `z_i` over (cold, stage)) and `alpha` as a `[value]` grid. [alpha.py:get_alpha_values] [alpha.py:_set_postoptimisation_flow_fractions]
- Writes solver results, logs and many attributes onto the model passed in (`mSuccess`, `solver_run`, `area_*`, `LMTD_*`, `z_allowed`). [execution.py:_solve_and_post_process] [area.py:set_utility_lmtd_and_areas]
- Segmented recovery areas and their dQ/dA size each match on its branch share of each stream's flow, so matches that split a stream in one stage are not sized on the whole stream's profile: a non-isothermal model's solved splits (`X`, `Y`), else the duty share isothermal mixing gives (`split_shares`). [area.py:_apply_segment_recovery_areas, _segment_exact_dqda]
- Lazy imports of `OpenPinch.analysis.heat_exchanger_networks.solver.piecewise` inside segmented-stream branches. [area.py:_apply_segment_recovery_areas] [piecewise.py:_utility_cost_expression]
- Emits log records at info, warning and error level. [execution.py:output_to_cmd_line] [alpha.py:get_alpha_values]
