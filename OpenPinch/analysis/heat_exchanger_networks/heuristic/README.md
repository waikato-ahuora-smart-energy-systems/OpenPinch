# HEN Heuristic Pinch Design

## Overview
A rules-based (Linnhoff) pinch design method. It proposes network topologies for each pinch side; the pinch design side model in `../models` realises each proposal with only its matches allowed. This package is pure Python decision logic: it reads a set-up, unsolved `PinchDecompModel` and builds no model of its own.

## Interface
- `pinch_side`, `PinchSide`, `SideStream`, `PINCH_TOLERANCE` (`side_streams.py`): one side's decision data. Each active stream carries per-period loads, the CP at its pinch end, whether it reaches the pinch, and its temperature against the heat counted from its pinch end; the side carries pair approaches, the recovery pairs the side model can place, and the utilities each stream can use.

- `pinch_regions`, `CASCADE_TOLERANCE`, `ISOTHERMAL_SPAN` (`regions.py`): a single-period side split at every pinch inside it into regions, nearest the pinch first.

- `pinch_streams`, `number_rule_met`, `cp_rule_met`, `split_counts`, `cp_feasible`, `CP_TOLERANCE` (`rules.py`): the Linnhoff pinch rules. The "must" streams are those with no utility on the side (hot above the pinch, cold below) and their partners the other kind; `split_counts` gives the branches per stream after the splitting rules, and `cp_feasible` whether split ratios exist that meet the CP rule on a set of pinch matches.
- `cp_distance` (`enumeration.py`): `|ln(CP_a / CP_b)|`, the CP dissimilarity used to order matches; similar CPs are a preference, not a rule.
- `propose_side_topologies`, `pinch_assignments`, `SideProposal`, `ProposedMatch`, `LOAD_FRACTION_TOLERANCE` (`enumeration.py`): up to a cap of distinct side topologies. A `SideProposal` lists its recovery matches (with tick-off duties) in a stage grid, the allowed heaters and coolers, its stage count and whether every must-stream load was placed; `z_restriction` gives the `[z, z_hu, z_cu]` restrictions the side model takes.

## Invariants
- Only streams active on the side in some period are listed. A stream absent in a period has zero load, zero CP and no pinch contact there [side_streams.py:_side_stream].
- The pinch end is a hot stream's outlet above the pinch and its inlet below; a cold stream's inlet above and its outlet below. A stream reaches the pinch in a period when its pinch-end temperature is within `PINCH_TOLERANCE` K of the pinch temperature shifted by its own contribution [side_streams.py:_side_stream].
- A segmented stream's pinch CP is that of the clipped segment at its pinch end, and its temperature follows the clipped piecewise profile [side_streams.py:_profile_points].
- `SideStream.cp_at` is the period-weighted CP of the profile segment at a heat position from the pinch end, each period's position scaled by its load [side_streams.py:SideStream.cp_at].
- `PinchSide.period_view` and `SideStream.period_view` give one period as single-period data [side_streams.py:PinchSide.period_view].
- A side model that holds its utility levels (`staged_utilities`) lists each level with a target in some period as a stream of the side's utility kind (hot above the pinch, cold below), indexed after the process streams (`I + u`, `J + u`), loaded with the level's target per period and running from its outlet (the pinch end) to its inlet. It is a must stream like the process streams of its kind. A level may match any partner (`match_allowed`; the match duty checks temperatures, as a staged unit can serve a stream between stages); its approach is the heater's or cooler's (`dT_hu_period`, `dT_cu_period`). `hot_count` and `cold_count` stay the process counts [side_streams.py:pinch_side, _level_streams, _level_approach].
- `SideStream.contributions` holds each period's temperature contribution; `SideStream.shifted` gives the profile's shifted temperatures [side_streams.py:SideStream.shifted].
- Pinches inside a side (a utility pinch of a held level, the level of a heat pump stream sized to its pocket, a second process pinch): a single-period side's cascade (the heat crossing a shifted temperature toward the pinch: hot beyond it less cold beyond it above the pinch, cold less hot below, plus the side's own utility, the duty its streams are out of balance by, which enters beyond every stream) is checked at every profile point strictly inside the side with streams on both sides of it; where it is within `CASCADE_TOLERANCE` (1e-5) of the side's load it closes, one cut per closed stretch. A stream spanning at most `ISOTHERMAL_SPAN` (0.02 K: a condenser or evaporator written with a glide) also closes the cascade at its far end when the crossing at its near end less its own heat is within the tolerance, as a target that sized it as a point at that end left it [regions.py:_level_crossings]. Each stream is sliced at the cuts (heat re-counted from each region's end nearer the pinch, pinch CP the slice's first segment, at the region's pinch when it starts at the cut) and each region with streams is a side of its own [regions.py:pinch_regions, _interior_pinches, _crossing, _slice].
- `load` and `pinch_cp` are period-weighted means; `pinch_cp` averages only over periods where the stream is active [side_streams.py:SideStream].

- Rules apply only to streams that reach the pinch. The CP rule for a pinch match holds when the must stream's CP is at most its partner's, within `CP_TOLERANCE`, in every period both reach the pinch [rules.py:cp_rule_met].
- Splitting: a must stream that no partner can take is split into the fewest branches (at least two) whose largest partner CPs cover its CP, then while must branches outnumber partner branches the partner with the largest CP per branch gains a branch [rules.py:split_counts, _branches_covering].
- `cp_feasible` checks each period separately as a transport problem (max flow): every must stream's CP shared over its pinch matches with no partner over its own CP [rules.py:cp_feasible].

- Pinch matches: each must stream takes as many partners as `split_counts` gives it branches and a partner hosts at most its branch count, with split ratios meeting the CP rule (`cp_feasible`); sets with fewer splits come first, then the most similar CPs (smallest summed `cp_distance` over the set; ties by the matches). Each must stream's allowed partners are ranked (CP rule met, then CP similarity) and at most 64 of their combinations are tried per search node. Up to four times the cap are collected within the node budget before this ordering, so the cap keeps the most CP-similar sets. If none exists, each partner may host one more branch, then the CP rule is dropped; proposals from such a set are marked incomplete [enumeration.py:_single_period_topologies]. Sets with one must stream split once more (largest CP first) follow as alternatives [enumeration.py:pinch_assignments].
- Tick-off: a pinch match takes the smaller of its two loads; a split stream shares its load over its pinch matches in proportion to the other streams' CPs. The side model mixes split branches at one temperature, so each pinch match is checked against the streams' temperatures at both ends of the stage (each stream's whole stage duty); when a split's match fails, every pinch match is also allowed one stage further out, for the side model to share the duty, and the proposal is marked incomplete (most such proposals cannot be realised) [enumeration.py:_tick_off, _stage_feasible].
- Away from the pinch, the must stream whose next unmatched heat is nearest the pinch is matched next (then the largest remaining load), so it takes the partners' slices nearest the pinch before a stream far from it uses them up, branching over partners that tick the stream off first, then in order of CP similarity at the matched ends (`SideStream.cp_at`, the segment CP there, period-weighted), then larger duty, then index. Tick-off comes before CP similarity: with CP first, every below-pinch proposal for the pulp mill left a cold load with no partner. A pair is matched again, as a second exchanger further out, only when no new partner can take the must stream's load. A match's duty is the largest, up to both remaining loads, whose two ends keep the pair's minimum approach in every period both streams run, each stream's slice taken next from its pinch end and scaled by its period load [enumeration.py:_extend, _candidates, _distance_from_pinch, _feasible_duty].
- Duties are found exactly: the margins (temperature difference less approach) are linear in the duty between the streams' profile breakpoints, so the pieces are searched from the top and each piece's zero crossings bound its feasible duties; a feasible stretch past an infeasible one (segmented streams) is found, and a parallel match need not be feasible at zero duty [enumeration.py:_largest_feasible, _profile_breakpoints, _feasible_duty, _parallel_duty].
- Stages: pinch matches sit next to the pinch (stage S - 1 above, stage 0 below) and every further match one stage further out than the last match on either of its streams. Every partner gets every utility it may use, whether or not the matches leave it load, so load the side model cannot place has somewhere to go [enumeration.py:_place, _proposal].
- Regions: a side split at the pinches inside it has each region designed on its own (`_region_topologies`, the search below); a proposal stacks one design per region, nearest the pinch first (above the pinch the nearest region takes the highest stages), taken best-first by summed rank like the multiperiod unions, and is complete when every region's design is [enumeration.py:_single_period_topologies, _stack, _best_first].
- A level's matches are dropped from the proposal (its partners may use every utility they may use); their stages stay in the grid, so the side model can place the level as a staged heater or cooler between its neighbours [enumeration.py:_proposal].
- Multiperiod: each period is designed on its own side view (`PinchSide.period_view`, its active streams with that period's data), and a proposal is the union of one design per period, stages aligned by distance from the pinch and heaters and coolers merged. Combinations are taken best-first by the sum of the designs' ranks; complete unions come first [enumeration.py:propose_side_topologies, _union].
- Loads within `LOAD_FRACTION_TOLERANCE` of a stream's load count as met. Proposals are de-duplicated by `signature`; each pinch-match set has its own depth-first search and the searches take turns; complete proposals come first and incomplete ones only fill the cap. The search on a side shares one node budget, per proposal requested, across its pinch-match searches, and keeps at most the cap of incomplete proposals; once those are kept, it stops after 1000 search nodes with no complete one [enumeration.py:propose_side_topologies, _single_period_topologies, _search].
- Fallback ladder: while a single-period side has fewer complete proposals than the cap, it is searched again by each fallback rung in turn, least intrusive first, each with its own node budget and stall limit; a rung's new complete proposals (by `signature`) are appended after those found so far, and the incomplete ones of the base search still only fill the cap. A side the base search fills runs no fallback [enumeration.py:_single_period_topologies, _FALLBACK_RUNGS].
  - Backtracking: each search node tries the three open must streams nearest the pinch in turn, not just the nearest. Matching the nearest first can let it take a partner's slice near the pinch that a hotter must stream needed (Ten-stream Ahmad above the pinch) [enumeration.py:_extend].
  - Split away from the pinch: a must stream no partner can take in series is matched in parallel with a partner's last match, in the later of the two streams' last stages, at the largest duty that keeps every match in that stage at its approach at both stage ends, each stream spanning its whole duty in the stage (mixed branches leave at one temperature). Without that check most such proposals cannot be realised. With no such partner the proposal is incomplete (Nine-stream above the pinch) [enumeration.py:_split_partner, _parallel_duty, _stage_feasible].

## Side Effects
- None: reads model attributes and solver arrays only. `../models/problem.py` (`InternalHeatExchangerNetworkProblem._solve_heuristic_pdm`) realises the proposals.
